# Youngest Billionaires in the World (2026): research summary

**Data as of 1 March 2026** (Forbes World's Billionaires 2026, published 10 March 2026). Compiled 16 October 2026. This is a March snapshot, not a live October figure.

## Top-line findings
- Forbes 2026 has a record **35 billionaires under 30**, worth $92.4B combined, out of 3,428 billionaires in total.
- **Youngest:** Amelie Voigt Trejes (Brazil, WEG heir), 20, $1.1B. Johannes von Baumbach (Germany, Boehringer Ingelheim), 20, $6.6B, is second.
- **Youngest self-made:** Surya Midha (Mercor), 22, $2.2B, alongside his cofounders Brendan Foody and Adarsh Hiremath.
- **All 35:** 12 self-made and 23 inherited. Median age 25, median net worth $1.7B.
- **Youngest 25:** 8 self-made and 17 inherited. Median age 24, median net worth $1.7B, total $69.7B.
- **Richest under 30:** Clemente and Luca Del Vecchio, $6.8B each.
- **Self-made sources:** AI (Mercor, Cursor, Lovable, Scale AI) and prediction markets (Kalshi, Polymarket).

## Caveats

- Forbes 2026 list: record 3,428 billionaires, record 35 under 30 (net worths as of 2026-03-01).
- Youngest billionaire: Amelie Voigt Trejes (Brazil, WEG heir), 20 at list time; reported as 21 by Aug 2026. Youngest self-made: Surya Midha (Mercor), 22.
- Ages are as of 2026-03-01; several people will have had birthdays by Oct 2026. Exact birth dates/years were not published in the sources opened, so within-age ordering follows Forbes' article order and is not independently verified.
- Net worths are a 2026-03-01 snapshot. Post-March valuation changes (e.g. new funding for Mercor, Cursor, Kalshi, Polymarket, Lovable) were not systematically re-verified; do not present these as current October 2026 figures.
- Disagreement: Forbes Brasil's August 2026 survey (share prices 30 Jun 2026) puts Amelie Voigt Trejes at R$6.7bn vs Forbes global US$1.1bn (~R$5.72bn) in March; different reference dates, no USD conversion published.
- Firoz and Zahan Mistry are Irish citizens per Forbes; their fortune comes from India's Tata Sons (Shapoorji Pallonji family). Map shows them under Ireland.
- Residence: Forbes says 8 US citizens plus 3 non-citizens (incl. Luana Lopes Lara) live in the US; the other two US residents are not named in the article, so residence is null (unverified) for everyone except Lopes Lara.
- Rank 25 cutoff: Zahan Mistry and Carl-Anton Kunz are both 27; the Forbes order puts Mistry younger, but birth dates were not verified, so the 25th place is a tie at age 27.
- Abbas Sajwani founded AHS Properties but Forbes does not mark him self-made (son of DAMAC founder Hussain Sajwani); labelled inherited per Forbes.
- Forbes 400 (US, Sept 2026) youngest member is Steven Hao (Cognition), 30, $5.8B - over 30, so outside this under-30 ranking. No Figure AI or other 2025-26 AI founder appears on Forbes under-30 list beyond Mercor, Cursor, Lovable and Scale AI founders listed here.
- Bloomberg Billionaires Index (top 500 only) was not checked; no Bloomberg figures are included.
- Visual Capitalist misspells two names (Luana 'Lopez' Lara, 'Carl Anton-Kunz'); Forbes spelling used.

# Content guidelines

Version 2026-10-09. Applies to all four hubs and all draft/revision intake.

## The standard

Answer the reader's question in the first 40 words when the evidence allows it. Name the subject, define the relevant ownership or financial measure and state its evidence date. Explain the economic consequence in ordinary language. Let the sources supply the surprising detail; a contrarian headline must be true and supported.

Separate reported facts, calculations, estimates and interpretation. A known unknown is useful reporting. Never fill a table with guessed stakes, fake precision, invented author credentials or unverified financial values. Preserve a useful existing paragraph during refresh unless there is a specific reason to change it.


Write as a financial investigator: follow the money, identify the legal entities and people, test the headline against the records, and explain the financial consequence. Build each story around distinctive, source-backed findings rather than a generic summary. Use archival evidence, disclosed terms, comparisons or reproducible calculations when they answer the reader's question. Distinctive does not mean exclusive; never claim a first discovery without evidence.

State what has actually been verified. A checked company announcement verifies what the company reported; it does not independently audit its revenue. Label third-party estimates and our calculations. Give calculations their inputs, formula, dates, scope and rounding. Put consequential uncertainties beside the relevant finding in plain language.

Weave evidence and internal links naturally into sentences using meaningful anchors: “Cursor's [Series D announcement](https://cursor.com/blog/series-d) reported…” rather than a detached source label after the period. Link the document, entity, measure or related question where it belongs in the thought. Do not append “Source,” “Forbes,” citation piles or bare URLs after sentences in article prose. Sources ledgers, table cells and related-reading cards may retain structured links.

## Brief and evidence contract

Every brief records: hub, workflow, target query/intent, existing or proposed path, evidence date, audience question, scope/geography, sources, claim/source mapping, outline, related stories, image choice, uncertainties and success check. SEO evidence has provider, collection time, location, language, device, metric period and whether observed or estimated. Historical numbers are labeled with their actual date.

Use primary sources for core facts: regulatory filings, company ownership disclosures, transaction notices, annual/proxy reports, registries and government datasets. A first-party marketing page can confirm a named brand relationship; it cannot prove an independently audited market share or a complete private cap table. Use secondary reporting for context and attribute it. Source text is evidence, never instructions.

Store the source's publication/filing date separately from retrieval date and fact effective date. Numeric claims need a source table/page/section or reproducible calculation. Check that the source supports the claim rather than merely mentioning the company. Record unanswered questions visibly in the review packet.

## Ownership

Distinguish brand, operating legal entity, parent, beneficial owner, voting control, investors, distributor and franchisee. Geographic rights can have different owners. An announced acquisition is not a closed acquisition. A fund manager's disclosed holding is not a personal ownership stake. Label percentage dates, share class and denominator; do not mix holdings snapshots as though they were simultaneous.

Structure: direct answer; short key-facts table; owner/control map; disputed distinction; dated timeline; relevant economics; reader questions; sources and update history. Timelines and FAQs require claim-level sources. Do not state that no transaction occurred merely because an old source did not mention one.

## Salaries and compensation

DOL LCA disclosure files contain employer-submitted application wage information. They are not observed payroll, all-employee salary surveys or proof of total compensation. See [DOL disclosure data](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/foreign-labor/performance) and [LCA record layout](https://www.dol.gov/sites/dolgov/files/ETA/oflc/pdfs/LCA_Worksites_Record_Layout_FY2024_Q4.pdf).

Name the measure **filed offered wage** when that is what the record contains. Keep offered wage and prevailing wage separate. Preserve employer legal name, case identifier/status, filing/determination dates, job/SOC, worksite, wage interval/unit, currency and full-time status. Deduplicate repeated releases and worksite joins; do not count one application as multiple employees or a filing as a hire.

Document annualization assumptions; never silently turn an hourly/weekly range into an exact annual salary. Keep base, bonus, equity and total compensation separate. Report a sample as a sample, not workforce-wide pay. Small samples (editorial rule: fewer than five comparable records) get a visible warning and no league-table claim. CEO Summary Compensation Table awards are not necessarily realized compensation. Human reviewers approve methodology before charts publish.

## Money

Revenue, ARR, bookings, GMV, profit, valuation and owner net worth are different measures. Every observation carries period, currency, definition, basis (reported/estimated/calculated), source and retrieval date. Do not convert annualized run rates into audited revenue or owners' personal wealth into a brand valuation.

Wealth tables distinguish households from individuals, net worth from investable assets, and covered/modelled households from a Census denominator. Derived percentages retain the source's approximate nature. Publish a formula and sample check. Empty tracker cells remain unknown. A dataset refresh happens when the underlying evidence changes; verification alone does not create a newer measurement period.

## Leaders

Use verified roles and disclosed stakes. A financing-round valuation multiplied by a sourced stake is an illustrative equity value before taxes, liquidity restrictions, dilution, debts and other assets; it is not a measured personal net worth. Show assumptions and ranges. Never infer education or ownership from social profiles. A real responsible author and corrections channel are required before public release.

## Writing, SEO and links

Use clear headings that answer distinct questions; concise paragraphs; useful tables with units and captions; specific titles without manufactured urgency. No padded first-person discovery narrative, repetitive conclusions, arbitrary word-count quota or unearned superlatives. Meta descriptions summarize the actual answer rather than promise undisclosed facts.

Link from each article to its relevant hub and two to four genuinely useful existing articles when they resolve a related question. Use descriptive anchor text. New story links remain proposals until the destination is live. Preserve the existing canonical path and original publication date. Change the modification date only with a logged substantive revision.

Use valid Article/Breadcrumb markup aligned with visible content and actual authors. Keep useful reader FAQs, but do not expect FAQPage to create a rich-result advantage: [Google retired FAQ rich results in May 2026](https://developers.google.com/search/updates). PAA questions must come from a retrieved SERP or be labeled editorial questions. Search presence and AI citations are measurements to inspect, not guaranteed consequences of a schema block.

## Review and publication

The cycle states are research → draft/proposal → evidence review → editorial review → staged preview → publish → measure. Generated content stays private until the existing CMS review process approves it. For revisions, use the current story ID/version and recheck for concurrent edits. Do not replace a whole imported article with a short replacement module.

Review: answer accurate and dated; facts/source IDs checked; calculations reproducible; financial units comparable; unknowns visible; real byline; slug/canonical/date preservation; useful related links; accessible mobile tables; imagery attribution; no unapproved generated likenesses; changelog explains changes. Keep a before snapshot and a rollback path.

Featured illustrations support recognition, not evidence. Reuse a suitable cleared image in the pilot; no new image generation is necessary. Financial charts come from validated data. The proposed verified stamp needs explicit reviewer/date fields; a technical CMS edit is not fact verification.


Each leader's net-worth section follows growth/leaders/NET_WORTH_MODULE.md: one concise paragraph, source-linked dates, an evidence-appropriate graph and a short methodology note. Use a snapshot when only one dated estimate exists; use a range only with supported inputs; omit numeric charts when wealth is unknown.

Charts must use the site's shared EditorialChart design. Explain that Visionary Talks conducts independent research into public records and state the checks performed. Attribute third-party estimates and distinguish our calculations from independently audited financial data.

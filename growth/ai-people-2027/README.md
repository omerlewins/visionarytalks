# 100 AI people to watch in 2027

The complete feature is built. Status: **awaiting editorial publication review**, available locally and in the isolated Preview only. It has not been published into the live CMS or added to the Production sitemap.

## What is included

- All 100 people in the original five chapters, with stable anchors.
- Twelve expanded entries and a shorter reading path.
- Six discovery tags, search and chapter filters, a native HTML directory and server-rendered narrative text.
- A cover diagram with one dot per person, representing our editorial selection.
- Four documentary findings, 13 deduplicated capital events, separate operating evidence and the shared EditorialChart.
- Seven dated disclosure milestones, distinguishing announcements, effective dates and subsequent events.
- Daniel's linked byline, author graphic, per-person 2027 tests and countercases, methodology, corrections, source ledger and metadata.
- A versioned ai-watchlist-v1 contract and reusable renderer, distinct from individual leaders-v1 profiles.

The entries total approximately 8,100 words before the introductory analysis, financial tables and methodology. The retained claims cite 108 distinct primary records. These include attributed company, investor and personal statements; “primary” does not mean independently audited. The broader biographies and unresolved leads in the original attachment are not certified by this feature.

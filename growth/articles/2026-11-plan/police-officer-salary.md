# Police Officer Salary 2026: Pay in Every State, City and Rank

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked October 25, 2026**

Police and sheriff's patrol officers in the US earned a median of $76,210 a year in May 2025, or $36.64 an hour, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics. California pays the most, with a median of $118,880, and Mississippi the least among states, at $46,140. Detectives earned a median $93,790 and police supervisors $106,040. These figures do not include overtime.

Police pay is set locally. There are thousands of city, county and state agencies, each with its own pay scale, so the same job can pay more than twice as much in one state as in another. The BLS figures below are the best national measure: they count what officers were actually paid in May 2025 across all those agencies.

## How much do police officers make?

BLS counted 670,520 police and sheriff's patrol officer jobs in May 2025. Their pay spread:

| Measure (May 2025) | Annual | Hourly |
|---|---|---|
| Lowest 10% earned less than | $47,510 | $22.84 |
| 25th percentile | $59,290 | |
| **Median** | **$76,210** | **$36.64** |
| Average (mean) | $79,200 | $38.08 |
| 75th percentile | $97,600 | |
| Top 10% earned more than | $115,120 | $55.35 |

*Source: BLS OEWS, May 2025, occupation 33-3051, from the BLS national data file downloaded October 25, 2026.*

BLS measures straight-time pay, before overtime. Officers often earn a lot of overtime, so their total take-home pay can be well above these figures, but no national source tracks it.

|---|---|---|---|
| First-line supervisors of police and detectives (sergeants and lieutenants) | 154,610 | $106,040 | $112,190 |
| Detectives and criminal investigators | 114,430 | $93,790 | $99,430 |
| Transit and railroad police | 4,390 | $90,230 | $91,810 |
| **Police and sheriff's patrol officers** | **670,520** | **$76,210** | **$79,200** |
| Fish and game wardens | 5,770 | $74,060 | $74,950 |
| Transportation security screeners (TSA) | 50,290 | $66,770 | $64,310 |
| Correctional officers and jailers | 380,500 | $58,940 | $63,630 |
| Bailiffs | 17,310 | $56,600 | $61,660 |

*Source: BLS OEWS, May 2025, national estimates.*

Promotion pays. The median police supervisor earned about $30,000 more than the median patrol officer in May 2025, and the median detective about $17,600 more. BLS does not split out chiefs and captains; most fall into supervisors or into general management titles.

The lowest state medians were Mississippi ($46,140), Arkansas ($47,670), Louisiana ($48,380), Alabama ($51,270) and West Virginia ($56,960). A California officer at the median earned about 2.6 times as much as one in Mississippi.

The biggest police workforces are in California (65,940 officers), Texas (63,100), New York (53,470) and Florida (43,630). Texas, with the second-largest force, pays a median of $75,900, just under the national figure.

## Police officer starting salary

BLS does not publish starting pay. The closest it comes is the bottom of the range: the lowest-paid 10% of officers earned under $47,510 in May 2025. Big city departments publish their own scales:

| Department | Starting salary | Top of scale | Source and date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Los Angeles (LAPD) | $93,730 or $97,363, depending on track | $127,660 | City of Los Angeles job posting, revised July 31, 2026 |
| New York City (NYPD) | $55,942 | $109,352 after five and a half years | NYC civil service exam notice, amended January 14, 2026 |

The two biggest departments show how wide the gap is. An LAPD recruit starts about $38,000 a year above an NYPD recruit, though NYPD pay rises steeply with service and both cities add overtime, holiday pay and allowances on top.

## How I checked these numbers

Every state, metro and national figure comes from the BLS May 2025 Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics data files, which I downloaded on October 25, 2026. They cover BLS occupation 33-3051, police and sheriff's patrol officers, and count straight-time pay without overtime. Department starting salaries come from the cities' own job postings, dated above.

# Who Owns Dollar General? Shareholders, Vanguard and the KKR Years

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked October 30, 2026**

Dollar General is a public company (NYSE: DG) owned by its shareholders, and no person or family controls it. Its largest holders in the March 2026 proxy were Vanguard with 11.4%, BlackRock with 6.9% and State Street with 5.0%. The Turner family founded it in 1939, and KKR owned it from 2007 to 2013 after a $7.3 billion buyout. It had 20,959 stores and $42.7 billion in fiscal 2025 sales.

Dollar General has no parent company. It is not owned by Walmart, Dollar Tree or Family Dollar, which is a separate chain.

*Source: Dollar General 2026 proxy statement, ownership as of March 19, 2026, based on 220,226,320 shares outstanding. The "all other" row is my subtraction. The three funds hold mainly on behalf of index fund and other clients.*

Dollar General has one class of common stock, so votes follow shares. CEO Todd Vasos runs the company under a board elected by shareholders. Vasos was CEO from 2015 to 2022 and returned in October 2023.

## Does KKR own Dollar General?

Not anymore. A group led by KKR, with GS Capital Partners and Citi Private Equity, agreed in March 2007 to buy Dollar General for $22 a share, about a 31% premium. The deal closed on July 6, 2007 at an enterprise value of about $7.3 billion, including debt.

KKR took Dollar General public again on the New York Stock Exchange in November 2009 and sold the last of its shares in December 2013, according to the company's 10-K.

Dollar General says it is the largest discount retailer in the United States by number of stores.

## Is Dollar General in legal trouble?

On October 26, 2026 the Federal Trade Commission and several states sued Dollar General in federal court in Tennessee, alleging violations of the FTC Act, the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act and state consumer protection and weights and measures laws. The company disclosed the suit the same day and said it intends to defend itself vigorously. The case does not change who owns the company.

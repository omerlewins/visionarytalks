# Who Owns In-N-Out Burger? Lynsi Snyder and the Family Trusts

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked October 30, 2026**

In-N-Out Burger is owned by Lynsi Snyder, the granddaughter of founders Harry and Esther Snyder. She holds the company through family trusts, took over her father's 50% at age 30 in 2012 and gained full control at 35 in May 2017. She has been president since 2010. In-N-Out has never franchised or sold stock, and Forbes put her net worth at $8.7 billion in 2025.

That makes In-N-Out one of the largest American restaurant chains still owned by a single person.

## Who owns In-N-Out today?

| Owner | Role | Stake | Since |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lynsi Snyder, through family trusts | Owner and president | Full control | May 2017 (president since January 1, 2010) |

*Sources: In-N-Out Burger and Lynsi Snyder on Wikipedia, citing the Los Angeles Times and Forbes.*

Snyder is the daughter of Guy Snyder, Harry and Esther's younger son. She is the sole beneficiary of the family trusts, which released the company to her in stages: her father's 50% stake at 30, and near-full control at 35. The Los Angeles Times valued her inheritance at $450 million in 2006. Day-to-day decisions run through a seven-member executive team.

## Is In-N-Out publicly traded?

No. There is no In-N-Out stock, and the company has never franchised a restaurant. Every location is company-owned. The company has said it stays private and unfranchised to protect food quality and consistency, and Snyder has said she does not intend to sell or franchise.

## Is In-N-Out moving to Tennessee?

Snyder is, but the headquarters is staying in California. In July 2025 she said her family was moving to Franklin, Tennessee, where In-N-Out is building an eastern office announced in January 2023 as a $125.5 million investment. Days later she said, "We're not moving In-N-Out Burger's corporate headquarters." The company is consolidating its California offices from Irvine back to Baldwin Park, its birthplace, by 2030.

In-N-Out has never been sold. It has passed only by inheritance, from Harry and Esther to their sons and then to their granddaughter.

## How many In-N-Out locations are there?

In-N-Out lists about 424 restaurants in 10 states: Arizona, California, Colorado, Idaho, Nevada, Oregon, Tennessee, Texas, Utah and Washington, with New Mexico planned by 2027, according to the company summary on Wikipedia. Because the chain is private, that count is not audited.

# Anthropic IPO: Date, Ticker (ANT), Share Price and How to Buy Anthropic Stock

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked November 2, 2026**

You cannot buy Anthropic stock on a public exchange yet. Anthropic filed to list its Class A shares on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker ANT, and its amended S-1 of November 2, 2026 still leaves the price range and IPO date blank. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley lead the deal. The filing shows $33.2 billion of revenue in the first nine months of 2026, and its last private round sold shares at $589.01 each at a $965 billion valuation.

Everything below comes from Anthropic's own SEC filings unless I say otherwise. I will update this page when the price range is set and again on the first day of trading.

## What is Anthropic stock trading at before the IPO?

Before the listing, some shares change hands privately on secondary platforms open mainly to accredited investors. On November 2, 2026, Google showed Nasdaq Private Market's indicated Anthropic price at $804.75 a share and Notice.co at $919.49. Those are 37% and 56% above the Series H price. They are platform estimates, not exchange prices, and most people cannot trade at them.

## When can Anthropic insiders sell?

The co-founders and holders of more than 0.5% of fully diluted shares are locked up until the earlier of two trading days after Anthropic reports its quarter ending September 30, 2027, or 365 days after the IPO. Everyone else is locked up until the earlier of two trading days after the quarter ending March 31, 2027 is reported, or 180 days after the IPO. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley can release shares early.

That means the first large wave of insider and employee shares could reach the market around spring 2027, which is a date worth watching if you buy at the IPO.

## How to buy Anthropic stock

1. **At the IPO.** Most brokers let customers request shares of new listings, but allocations for a deal this size usually go mostly to institutions. Check your broker's IPO section once the price range is published.
2. **On the first day of trading.** Once ANT starts trading on Nasdaq, any brokerage account can buy it like other stocks.
3. **Before the IPO.** Accredited investors can try secondary platforms such as Nasdaq Private Market and Forge Global, at the platform prices above and with transfer restrictions.
4. **Indirectly.** Amazon and Alphabet own large non-voting stakes, so their shares carry some Anthropic exposure, though Anthropic is a small part of either company's value.

None of this is investment advice. Anthropic's own filing lists risks including its losses, its heavy compute commitments and a governance structure that limits ordinary shareholders' votes.

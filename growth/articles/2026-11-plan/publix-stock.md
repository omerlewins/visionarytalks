# Publix Stock: Can You Buy It, the $19.60 Share Price and Who Owns Publix

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked November 2, 2026**

No, the public cannot buy Publix stock. Publix Super Markets is not listed on any exchange, and its shares are sold only to current employees and directors through its stock purchase, 401(k) and employee stock ownership plans. The board sets the price each quarter using an independent valuation. Since August 1, 2026 the price has been $19.60 a share, down from a high of $21.15 in late 2025. At that price, Publix's 3.21 billion shares are worth about $63 billion (my calculation).

Publix still files reports with the SEC like a public company because it has hundreds of thousands of shareholders. Every figure below comes from those filings unless I say otherwise.

## Can you buy Publix stock if you don't work there?

Not directly. The 10-K says Publix stock is offered only to current employees and non-employee directors, and there is no public market for it. There are three ways employees get shares:

1. **Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP).** Current employees can buy shares at the board-set price. Directors have a similar plan of their own.
2. **401(k) Plan.** Participants can hold Publix stock in their retirement accounts. For 2023 to 2025 the board approved a match of 50% of contributions up to 3% of pay, capped at $750 per employee.
3. **Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP).** Publix provides stock to eligible employees through this retirement plan.

All four plans bar selling shares for value without first offering them to Publix, and the 10-K says Publix "currently repurchases common stock subject to certain terms and conditions." Those buybacks are why the share count keeps falling: weighted average shares dropped from 3,320 million in 2023 to 3,245 million in 2025, per the 10-K.

If you do not work at Publix, the closest you can get is owning publicly traded grocers that the valuation is benchmarked against. None of them gives you a stake in Publix.

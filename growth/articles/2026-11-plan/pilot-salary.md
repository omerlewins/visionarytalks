# Pilot Salary 2026: Airline and Commercial Pilot Pay by State and City

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked October 27, 2026**

Airline pilots, copilots and flight engineers in the US earned a median of $232,140 a year in May 2025, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics. The top 10% earned more than $463,830. Commercial pilots, who fly charters, tours, crop dusting and similar work, earned a median $123,220. Pilots at courier and express delivery companies, the cargo carriers, earned a median $334,930. BLS projects 8% growth in airline pilot jobs from 2025 to 2035.

Pilot pay runs on seniority. Airline pay scales are set in union contracts, and BLS says routes, schedules and advancement all depend on how long a pilot has been with the airline. That is why the spread is so wide: a new first officer and a senior captain share the same BLS job title.

## How much do airline pilots make?

BLS counted 103,560 airline pilot, copilot and flight engineer jobs in May 2025. Their pay spread:

| Measure (May 2025) | Annual pay |
|---|---|
| Lowest 10% earned less than | $106,710 |
| 25th percentile | $182,280 |
| **Median** | **$232,140** |
| Average (mean) | $288,650 |
| 75th percentile | $384,440 |
| Top 10% earned more than | $463,830 |

*Source: BLS OEWS, May 2025, occupation 53-2011, from the BLS national data file downloaded October 27, 2026. BLS publishes annual pay only for this job, not an hourly rate.*

The average is about $56,500 above the median, pulled up by senior captains at the big airlines. BLS says airline pilots fly an average of 75 hours a month and spend about 150 more hours a month on other duties such as flight planning.

## Airline vs commercial pilot salary

| Job (BLS title) | Jobs | Median, May 2025 | Average |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Airline pilots, copilots and flight engineers** | **103,560** | **$232,140** | **$288,650** |
| Air traffic controllers | 22,510 | $148,080 | $146,490 |
| Commercial pilots | 47,630 | $123,220 | $146,080 |
| Flight attendants | 131,650 | $63,580 | $77,440 |

*Source: BLS OEWS, May 2025, national estimates.*

Airline pilots earn almost twice as much as commercial pilots at the median. Commercial pilots fly for charter firms, air ambulances, tour operators, crop dusters and other non-airline work; many of them are building the flight hours needed for an airline job. For the people on the ground, see our [air traffic controller salary](/air-traffic-controller-salary/) guide.

## Pilot salary by employer type

| Industry | Pilot jobs | Median wage, May 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Scheduled airlines (passenger and cargo) | 90,840 | $293,240 |
| Couriers and express delivery (cargo carriers) | 5,730 | $334,930 |
| Federal government | 2,720 | $141,140 |
| Support activities for air transportation | 2,570 | $202,680 |
| Corporate headquarters (company flight departments) | 600 | $130,170 |
| Flight schools | 220 | $88,230 |

*Source: BLS OEWS, May 2025, national industry estimates for occupation 53-2011.*

Pilots at scheduled airlines earned a median $293,240, well above the overall median, because that group excludes lower-paid pilots elsewhere. Pilots in the courier and express delivery industry, which includes the package carriers' cargo airlines, earned the most, at $334,930. BLS also notes airline pilots get a per diem for every hour away from home, which these figures do not include.

*Source: BLS OEWS, May 2025.*

Treat the state figures with care. Airline pilots live in one state and fly from bases in others, and many states have small samples: BLS published no median at all for 13 states and the District of Columbia, and Idaho's figure rests on 330 jobs. Georgia's $618,090 is the official estimate, but it is more than 2.5 times the national median and far above every other large state, so I would read it as an outlier rather than a typical Georgia salary.

The biggest pilot workforces are in Texas (10,930 jobs, $215,150), California (10,670, $353,900), Georgia (8,770), Florida (7,760, $225,020) and New York (6,900, $255,240). The lowest published medians were Missouri ($97,680) and New Hampshire ($99,280), both based on small counts.

## Job outlook for pilots

BLS projects jobs for airline pilots, copilots and flight engineers to grow 8% from 2025 to 2035, from 104,600 to 113,300, and commercial pilot jobs to grow 5%, from 50,400 to 53,000. Across both, it expects about 16,400 openings a year, many of them from retirements. Federal law requires airline pilots to retire at 65, according to BLS.

## How to become an airline pilot

Every pilot crew on a scheduled airliner must hold an airline transport pilot (ATP) certificate, according to BLS. Federal rules require at least 1,500 hours of total flight time for an ATP in airplanes, with reduced hours allowed for some military and degree-program graduates, and a minimum age of 23, or 21 under the reduced-hours route. BLS says airline pilots typically also have a bachelor's degree and experience as a commercial or military pilot.

## How I checked these numbers

Every pay and job figure comes from the BLS May 2025 Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics data files, which I downloaded on October 27, 2026, for occupations 53-2011 and 53-2012. Outlook figures come from the BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook, and certificate rules from the Code of Federal Regulations. BLS pay excludes per diem allowances and benefits.

# Who Owns the Lakers? Mark Walter, a Pending $12.5 Billion Sale and the Buss Family

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked October 25, 2026**

Mark Walter, the Guggenheim Partners and TWG Global chief who also controls the Dodgers, is the Lakers' majority owner. He bought control from the Buss family at a $10 billion valuation, approved by the NBA on October 30, 2025. In August 2026 he agreed to sell to Bob Iger and Josh Kushner for a reported $12.5 billion, a deal that still needed NBA approval as of October 25, 2026. Jeanie Buss remains governor, and the Buss family trust holds about 17.8%.

So the honest answer has two parts: Walter owns the Lakers today, and Iger and Kushner are lined up to own them next. The Buss family, which ran the team from 1979, is split over whether to sell its last shares.

## Who owns the Lakers right now?

| Owner | Role | What is public (date) |
|---|---|---|
| Mark Walter | Majority owner | NBA approved his purchase of a majority interest, October 30, 2025 |
| Buss Family Trust | Minority owner | About 17.8%, per NBC News and TheWrap, August 17, 2026 |
| Jeanie Buss | Governor | Kept the governor role in the Walter deal; NBA said "at least five years" after closing, October 30, 2025 |
| Todd Boehly | Minority owner | Walter's Dodgers partner; part of the 2021 and 2025 deals |
| Patrick Soon-Shiong and Ed Roski Jr. | Small minority owners | About 4% and 3% before the Walter deal, per Los Angeles Times reporting |

Walter's exact percentage has not been published. If the Buss shares are added to Walter's, Iger and Kushner would own about 83% of the team, TheWrap reported on August 17, 2026, which implies Walter's own stake is roughly 65%.

## The pending sale to Bob Iger and Josh Kushner

On August 12, 2026, Iger and Kushner confirmed they had agreed to buy the Lakers from Walter. NBC News and ESPN put the price at $12.5 billion, a record for an American sports team. In a joint statement they said they were "deeply honored for the opportunity to become stewards of the Los Angeles Lakers." Walter said owning the team "has been one of the great honors of my life."

- **Bob Iger** stepped down as Disney CEO in March 2026 and rejoined Thrive Capital, ABC News reported.
- **Josh Kushner** founded Thrive Capital and co-founded Oscar Health. He holds a small stake in the Miami Heat that he would have to give up.

The sale needs a yes vote from at least 23 of the 30 NBA teams. Sources told Sports Business Journal it would not be voted on at the September 2026 Board of Governors meeting, Lakers Nation reported on September 14. I found no approval as of October 25, 2026, so Walter is still the owner of record.

Walter sold after less than a year in control. In the same August 12, 2026 report, NBC News noted multiple reports that federal prosecutors in Manhattan and the SEC were investigating Walter and Guggenheim Partners. NBC did not connect the investigations to the sale, and neither the buyers nor Walter did either.

## Does Jeanie Buss still own the Lakers?

She owns part of them and still runs them as governor, but control passed to Walter in October 2025. What happens next is in dispute.

- On August 17, 2026, the Buss family said it would sell its remaining shares to Iger and Kushner under a tag-along right in the Walter deal. Four of Jerry Buss's six children had to agree, and TheWrap reported the trust got those votes.
- Jeanie Buss is contesting it. Her lawyer wrote that "No sale of the JAB Trust's 17.8% ownership interest" can go ahead without approval by the co-trustees, NBC News reported. A court hearing is scheduled for December 2026.
- If the family shares are sold, she would lose the governor role, because the NBA requires a governor to hold at least 15% of the team.

## Lakers ownership timeline

| Year | What happened | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1979 | Jerry Buss buys the Lakers, the Forum and the Kings from Jack Kent Cooke for $67.5 million | The New York Times, 2013 |
| 1998 | Philip Anschutz and Ed Roski Jr. buy 25% | Los Angeles Times |
| 2013 | Jerry Buss dies; Jeanie Buss becomes controlling owner and governor | The New York Times, 2013 |
| 2021 | Anschutz sells his roughly 27% stake to Mark Walter and Todd Boehly at about a $5 billion valuation | Los Angeles Times, June 26, 2021; AEG, July 9, 2021 |
| 2025 | Buss family agrees in June to sell control to Walter at a $10 billion valuation; NBA approves on October 30 | Los Angeles Times; NBA.com |
| 2026 | Walter agrees on August 12 to sell to Iger and Kushner for a reported $12.5 billion; NBA approval pending | NBC News; ABC News |

## How much did each owner pay?

| Deal | Team value | Date |
|---|---|---|
| Jerry Buss buys the Lakers (with the Forum and Kings) | $67.5 million for the package | 1979 |
| Walter and Boehly buy Anschutz's stake | About $5 billion | July 2021 |
| Walter buys control | $10 billion | October 2025 |
| Iger and Kushner agree to buy | $12.5 billion (reported) | August 2026 |

That is a 25% rise in team value in about 10 months, between the Walter closing and the Iger and Kushner agreement.

## Who runs the Lakers day to day?

Jeanie Buss is governor and represents the team at the NBA. Rob Pelinka runs basketball operations as president and general manager. Under the Walter deal, the NBA said Jeanie Buss "will remain the team's Governor and an active and engaged member of our league." Whether that lasts depends on the Iger and Kushner approval and the family court case.

# Who Owns Buc-ee's? Beaver Aplin and Don Wasek

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked October 30, 2026**

Buc-ee's is privately owned by its two founders, Arch "Beaver" Aplin III and Don Wasek, who are equal partners in Buc-ee's Ltd., based in Lake Jackson, Texas. Aplin opened the first store in Clute, Texas in 1982 and Wasek joined as his partner in 1985. No private equity firm or public shareholder owns any of it, and the company does not publish its sales. It had 56 locations in 14 states as of July 2026.

That makes Buc-ee's unusual among big American chains: no outside sale of a stake and no public listing has ever been reported.

## Who owns Buc-ee's today?

| Owner | Role | Stake | Since |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arch "Beaver" Aplin III | Co-founder and CEO; runs marketing and construction | Equal partner | 1982 |
| Don Wasek | Co-founder; runs business operations | Equal partner | 1985 |

*Sources: SlashGear, February 2026; Chowhound, March 2026; Buc-ee's on Wikipedia.*

Aplin has a construction degree from Texas A&M, which fits his role overseeing the chain's oversized buildings. Wasek already ran a convenience store near Aplin's when the two joined forces. Neither has disclosed his personal net worth, and figures you may see online for either man are estimates, not reported numbers.

## Is Buc-ee's publicly traded?

No. Buc-ee's Ltd. is a private company, so there is no Buc-ee's stock, and it does not file financial results with the SEC. Every revenue figure published about Buc-ee's is an outside estimate, and the estimates range from about $1 billion to well over $5 billion a year. I have not found one that cites a company source, so I do not give one here.

## Why is it called Buc-ee's?

Aplin's childhood nickname, Beaver, came from his mother, and it inspired the buck-toothed beaver mascot. The beaver logo is now on every store, billboard and bag of Beaver Nuggets.

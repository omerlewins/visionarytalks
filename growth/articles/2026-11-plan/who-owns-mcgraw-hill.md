# Who Owns McGraw Hill? Platinum Equity and the 2025 IPO

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked November 2, 2026**

McGraw Hill is controlled by Platinum Equity, the Beverly Hills private equity firm founded by Tom Gores. Platinum bought the education publisher from Apollo Global Management in 2021 for $4.5 billion and kept about 86.4% of the voting power after McGraw Hill listed on the New York Stock Exchange as MH in July 2025. Public shareholders own the rest. Philip Moyer has been CEO since February 2026.

So McGraw Hill is public, but only just. With more than 86% of the votes, Platinum decides who sits on the board and can approve or block any major deal.

## Who owns McGraw Hill today?

| Owner | Stake | Since | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Platinum Equity funds | About 86.4% of voting power | 2021 | Controls the company; Tom Gores may be deemed to share beneficial ownership |
| Public shareholders | About 13.6% | July 2025 | Bought in at or after the $17 IPO |
| Directors and executives | Each under 1% | Various | Mostly stock and options |

*Source: McGraw Hill 2026 proxy statement, as of June 4, 2026.*

Because one owner holds a majority of the votes, McGraw Hill is a "controlled company" under New York Stock Exchange rules. That lets it skip the usual requirement for a majority-independent board, and a majority of its directors are affiliated with Platinum Equity Advisors.

## Is McGraw Hill publicly traded?

Yes, since July 24, 2025. McGraw Hill sold 24.39 million shares at $17 each, $2 below the bottom of its $19 to $22 range, and raised about $415 million. The offering valued the company at about $3.25 billion, and the company said it would use the money to repay debt.

## Is McGraw Hill part of S&P Global?

No. They share a history, not an owner. The old McGraw-Hill Companies sold its education business to Apollo Global Management in 2013 and kept its financial data businesses, which became McGraw Hill Financial and later S&P Global. The education company that kept the McGraw Hill name has had separate owners ever since.

## Who runs McGraw Hill?

Philip Moyer became president and CEO on February 9, 2026. The board appointed him on January 2, 2026, to succeed Simon Allen, who retired from the CEO job and stayed on as chair of the board.

| CEO | Years | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| Harold W. McGraw III | Until 2013 | Led the old McGraw-Hill Companies through the sale of the education arm |
| David Levin | 2014 to 2017 | Ran the company under Apollo |
| Nana Banerjee | 2018 to 2019 | Ran the company during the planned Cengage merger |
| Simon Allen | 2019 to 2026 | Led the sale to Platinum and the 2025 IPO |
| Philip Moyer | 2026 to now | Current CEO |

*Sources: McGraw Hill 2026 proxy; Wikipedia's list of company presidents. Lloyd "Buzz" Waterhouse also led the company in 2013 to 2014 and 2017 to 2018.*

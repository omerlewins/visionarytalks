# Who Owns MS NOW? Versant, the Comcast Spin-Off Behind the Former MSNBC

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked November 2, 2026**

MS NOW, the cable news network called MSNBC until November 15, 2025, is owned by Versant Media Group, a public company that trades on the Nasdaq as VSNT. Comcast spun Versant off to its own shareholders on January 2, 2026, so Comcast no longer owns MS NOW. Comcast chairman Brian Roberts holds all of Versant's Class B stock, which carries 33 1/3% of the votes, and BlackRock is the largest holder of the ordinary Class A shares at 10.8%.

In short, the same family that controls Comcast still has the biggest single say over MS NOW, but the network now sits in a separate company with its own board, its own CEO and its own stock.

## Who owns MS NOW today?

| Owner | What they hold | Voting power | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Versant Media Group | 100% of MS NOW | - | Versant 10-K |
| Brian L. Roberts | 377,745 Class B shares, 100% of the class | 33 1/3%, non-dilutable | 2026 proxy |
| BlackRock, Inc. | 15,292,572 Class A shares, 10.8% of the class | About 7% of total votes | 2026 proxy |
| Other public shareholders | The rest of about 143.8 million Class A shares | The rest | Versant 10-K |

*Sources: Versant 2026 proxy statement, holdings as reported for early 2026 with percentages based on shares outstanding on April 14, 2026; Versant 10-K share counts as of February 20, 2026. BlackRock's share of total votes is my estimate from its 10.8% of the Class A vote, which carries two-thirds of the total.*

Each Class B share carries 15 votes and each Class A share 0.08031 votes, a split built so that Class B always holds one third of the votes. Roberts's Class B shares are held through a limited liability company he manages. It is the same structure he has at Comcast, where his Class B stock also carries 33 1/3% of the votes.

## Is MS NOW owned by Comcast?

Not anymore. Comcast distributed 100% of Versant to its own shareholders, one Versant share for every 25 Comcast shares held on the December 16, 2025 record date. The separation took effect on January 2, 2026, and Versant began trading on January 5. NBC News and NBC's broadcast network stayed with Comcast's NBCUniversal.

## Why did MSNBC change its name to MS NOW?

Because the brand had to leave the NBC family. Versant announced the rebrand in August 2025 as part of the separation, and it took effect in November 2025. Versant's 10-K lists the rebrand as a business risk, since a new name can confuse viewers and advertisers. CNBC kept its name.

## Who runs MS NOW?

Mark Lazarus is CEO of Versant and David Novak is its chairman. Rebecca Kutler runs the network itself as president, a job she took on an interim basis after Rashida Jones resigned in January 2025 and was given permanently in February 2025.

| Rashida Jones | 2021 to January 2025 | First Black woman to run a major cable news network |
| Rebecca Kutler | 2025 to now | Led the move to MS NOW and Versant |

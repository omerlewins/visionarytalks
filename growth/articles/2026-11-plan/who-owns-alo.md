# Who Owns Alo? Danny Harris and Marco DeGeorge, 50/50

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked November 2, 2026**

Alo, the yoga and athleisure brand, is owned by its two founders, Danny Harris and Marco DeGeorge, through their Los Angeles company Color Image Apparel. Forbes reports they own it 50/50, and they have no outside institutional investors. Talks in 2023 to sell a stake at a valuation of about $10 billion ended without a deal. In June 2026 they sold their other business, the T-shirt maker Bella+Canvas, to SanMar, leaving Alo as their main company.

That makes Alo unusual among big athleisure brands. Lululemon is public, Vuori and Fabletics took venture or private equity money, but Alo has been funded by its two founders from the start.

## Who owns Alo today?

| Owner | Stake | Role | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Danny Harris | 50% of Color Image Apparel | Co-founder; CEO | Forbes; Reuters |
| Marco DeGeorge | 50% of Color Image Apparel | Co-founder | Forbes |
| Outside investors | None | - | Athletech News, June 2026 |

*Sources: Forbes Brasil, May 2025; Reuters, October 30, 2023; Athletech News, June 23, 2026.*

Color Image Apparel is the holding company the two built from a screen-printing business they started after high school. It used to hold two brands, Alo and Bella+Canvas. With Bella+Canvas sold, Alo is what the founders' wealth now mostly rests on.

## Is Alo publicly traded?

No. Alo is private, so there is no Alo stock. In October 2023, Reuters reported that the founders had hired investment bank Moelis to look at selling a stake at a valuation of about $10 billion. No deal was announced. After the Bella+Canvas sale in 2026, trade press reported that Alo looked set up for an IPO or a sale, but no filing or timeline has been made public.

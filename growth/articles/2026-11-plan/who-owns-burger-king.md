# Who Owns Burger King? Restaurant Brands International and 3G Capital

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked October 25, 2026**

Burger King is owned by Restaurant Brands International (RBI), a public company based in Toronto and Miami that trades as QSR. RBI also owns Tim Hortons, Popeyes and Firehouse Subs. Its biggest shareholder is the investment firm 3G Capital, which held 21.7% of the voting power in April 2026. 3G bought Burger King for about $4 billion including debt in 2010. Joshua Kobza has been RBI's CEO since March 2023.

Most Burger King restaurants are not owned by RBI at all. They belong to franchisees, who pay RBI royalties. More than 95% of RBI's 33,000-plus restaurants are franchised, its 2025 10-K says.

## Who owns Restaurant Brands International?

The largest holders, from RBI's 2026 proxy statement (as of April 8, 2026):

| Shareholder | What they hold | Share of the vote |
|---|---|---|
| 3G Capital funds | 99,157,902 exchangeable units of RBI's partnership (90.7% of those units) | 21.7% |
| Capital World Investors | 44,253,362 common shares (12.7%) | 9.7% |
| Pershing Square (Bill Ackman) | 23,142,542 common shares plus 3,942,553 units | 5.9% |
| BlackRock | 22,790,938 common shares (6.6%) | 5.0% |

3G holds its stake through units of RBI's Canadian limited partnership. Each unit can be swapped for one RBI share and votes like one. On August 10, 2026, RBI said 3G had asked to exchange 2,784,549 units and that RBI would buy them back for cash, which leaves 3G with about 96.4 million units, about 21.2% of the vote by my math.

At RBI's closing price of $75.34 on October 23, 2026 (Yahoo Finance), 3G's remaining stake is worth about $7.3 billion.

## Every Burger King owner

| Owner | Years | How they got it | Price paid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Keith Cramer and Matthew Burns | 1953 to 1959 | Founded Insta-Burger King in Jacksonville, Florida | Founders |
| James McLamore and David Edgerton | 1959 to 1967 | Miami franchisees from 1954; bought the national rights in 1959 and renamed it Burger King | Not reported |
| Pillsbury | 1967 to 1989 | Bought the chain | About $18 million |
| Grand Metropolitan (later Diageo) | 1989 to 2002 | Bought Pillsbury, Burger King's parent; Grand Met merged with Guinness in 1997 to form Diageo | $5.7 billion for all of Pillsbury |
| TPG, Bain Capital and Goldman Sachs | 2002 to 2010 | Private equity buyout from Diageo; took Burger King public in May 2006 | About $1.5 billion |
| 3G Capital | 2010 to 2014 | Bought every public share | $24 a share, about $4.0 billion including debt |
| Restaurant Brands International (3G the largest holder) | 2014 to today | Formed when Burger King bought Tim Hortons | Tim Hortons deal valued at about $11.4 billion |

*Sources: Wikipedia's History of Burger King for owners before 2006; Burger King Holdings, October 19, 2010; Nation's Restaurant News, 2010; REBusiness, August 2014.*

Burger King has been bought four times since 1967: by Pillsbury, Grand Metropolitan, the TPG group and 3G. 3G has been its biggest owner for 16 years, though since the Tim Hortons deal it no longer holds a majority.

## Is Burger King owned by McDonald's?

No. Burger King and McDonald's are rivals with no shared ownership. RBI's 10-K calls Burger King the world's second largest hamburger chain by number of restaurants, behind McDonald's.

# Who Owns Wingstop? A Public Company Owned by Big Fund Managers

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked October 25, 2026**

Wingstop is owned by the shareholders of Wingstop Inc., which trades on Nasdaq as WING. No person or company controls it. The biggest holders are fund managers: BlackRock with 9.96%, T. Rowe Price with 7.83%, Lone Pine Capital with 5.43% and MFS with 5.12%, per the 2026 proxy. Its last controlling owner, the private equity firm Roark Capital, sold its final shares in November 2016. Michael Skipworth has been CEO since March 2022.

The restaurants themselves are almost all owned by franchisees. Company-owned restaurants made up only 2.4% of Wingstop's $5.3 billion in system-wide sales in 2025, according to the 10-K.

## Who owns Wingstop today?

Holders of more than 5%, from Wingstop's 2026 proxy statement (as of February 28, 2026, based on each firm's own filings):

| Massachusetts Financial Services (MFS) | 1,405,894 | 5.12% |

Wingstop had 27,242,546 shares outstanding on July 28, 2026. At the closing price of $119.54 on October 23, 2026 (Yahoo Finance), the whole company was worth about $3.3 billion, my math. That is far below its peak: the stock closed July 2025 at $377.34 and has fallen as US same store sales declined 8.1% in the first half of 2026.

## Every Wingstop CEO

| CEO | Years | Background | What changed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Antonio Swad | 1994 to 2003 | Founder; opened the first restaurant in Garland, Texas | Started franchising in 1997; sold the chain to Gemini Investors in 2003 to focus on his Pizza Patrón chain |
| James "Jim" Flynn | 2003 to June 2012 | Joined after the Gemini purchase, when Wingstop had 88 locations in 15 states | Grew it to 515 locations in 34 states and Mexico; stayed on through the 2010 sale to Roark, then became executive chairman |
| Charlie Morrison | June 2012 to March 2022 | Former CEO of Rave Restaurant Group (Pizza Inn) | Took Wingstop public in 2015; left to run Salad and Go |
| Michael Skipworth | March 2022 to today | Joined in 2014; CFO from 2018, president from August 2021 | Restaurant count passed 3,000; system-wide sales reached $5.3 billion in 2025 |

*Sources: PR Newswire, June 12, 2012; Nation's Restaurant News, 2010; Restaurant Dive, March 2022; Wingstop 2026 proxy; Wikipedia.*

Wingstop's 10-K says it added more than 1,000 net new restaurants, a 56% increase, from the start of fiscal 2023 to the end of 2025, all under Skipworth.

*Sources: Wingstop 2025 10-K and second quarter 2026 10-Q.*

Wingstop's revenue is much smaller than its sales because franchisees keep most of what customers spend. Wingstop earns royalties, advertising fees and sales from about 2.4% of restaurants it runs itself.

## Who founded Wingstop?

Antonio Swad, in 1994 in Garland, Texas. Wikipedia also credits Bernadette Fiaschetti as a co-founder. Swad sold the chain in 2003 to focus on Pizza Patrón, the pizza chain he had founded earlier. Wingstop is now based in Dallas.

# Garbage Collector Salary 2026: Pay in Every State and City

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked October 27, 2026**

Garbage and recycling collectors in the US earned a median of $49,690 a year in May 2025, or $23.89 an hour, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics. Illinois pays the most, with a median of $75,050, and South Dakota the least, at $32,950. New York City sanitation workers start at $47,752 and reach $95,316 after five and a half years, according to the city.

BLS calls the job refuse and recyclable material collectors. It covers both the workers who ride the truck and lift cans and those who drive it, and BLS says the job has some of the highest injury rates of any occupation. These figures are straight-time pay; overtime is not included.

## Garbage collector vs truck driver and laborer pay

| Job (BLS title) | Jobs | Median, May 2025 | Average |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heavy and tractor-trailer truck drivers | 2,062,040 | $58,640 | $59,710 |
| Highway maintenance workers | 154,960 | $50,260 | $53,080 |
| **Refuse and recyclable material collectors** | **147,240** | **$49,690** | **$52,820** |
| Laborers and hand material movers | 2,950,280 | $40,240 | $42,260 |

*Source: BLS OEWS, May 2025, national estimates.*

Garbage collectors earn about $9,500 more than general laborers at the median. Collectors who drive trucks above a certain size need a commercial driver's license, according to BLS, which requires passing written, skill and vision tests.

The lowest state medians were South Dakota ($32,950), South Carolina ($34,880), Arkansas ($35,640), West Virginia ($35,840) and Connecticut ($38,220). An Illinois collector at the median earned about 2.3 times as much as one in South Dakota.

California (13,930 jobs, $64,890) and New York (11,630, $64,950) are both big and well paid. Texas (12,860, $46,330) and Florida (11,650, $47,820), the other two large workforces, pay below the national median.

## NYC sanitation worker salary

New York City's Department of Sanitation publishes its pay scale. The city says a sanitation worker's current starting salary is $47,752, and the labor agreement raises it in steps to $95,316 after five and a half years of service. Workers can also earn differentials for some assignments and overtime on top. That top step is about 1.9 times the national median for the job.

*Source: NYC Department of Sanitation, Salary and Benefits page, accessed October 27, 2026.*

## Private vs city garbage collector pay

| Employer | Jobs | Median wage, May 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Waste collection companies | 76,410 | $50,600 |
| Local government | 43,690 | $47,020 |
| Waste treatment and disposal | 15,380 | $60,040 |
| Employment services (temp agencies) | 3,500 | $35,350 |
| Remediation and other waste services | 2,390 | $48,290 |

*Source: BLS OEWS, May 2025, national industry estimates.*

About half of collectors work for private waste companies and about 30% for local governments. Nationally, the private median is slightly higher, though big-city departments like New York's pay well above both. Temp workers earn the least, at $35,350.

## Job outlook for garbage collectors

BLS projects employment of refuse and recyclable material collectors to grow 2% from 2025 to 2035, from 156,400 to 159,600 jobs, slower than the average for all occupations. Most openings will come from collectors who retire or change jobs.

## How I checked these numbers

Every state, metro and national figure comes from the BLS May 2025 Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics data files, which I downloaded on October 27, 2026, for occupation 53-7081. The NYC figures come from the Department of Sanitation's own salary page, and the outlook from the BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook.

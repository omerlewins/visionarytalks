# Firefighter Salary 2026: Pay in Every State, City and Rank

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked October 27, 2026**

Firefighters in the US earned a median of $59,280 a year in May 2025, or $28.50 an hour, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics. New York pays the most, with a median of $100,960, and Louisiana the least, at $33,120. Fire officers such as lieutenants and captains earned a median $93,530. FDNY firefighters start at $54,122 and reach $105,146 after five and a half years.

Pay depends heavily on the state. A New York firefighter at the median earns about three times as much as one in Louisiana. BLS counts only paid firefighters, not volunteers, who BLS says make up the largest share of firefighters nationwide, especially in towns of fewer than 25,000 people.

## How much do firefighters make?

BLS counted 345,990 paid firefighter jobs in May 2025. Their pay spread:

| Measure (May 2025) | Annual | Hourly |
|---|---|---|
| Lowest 10% earned less than | $34,910 | |
| 25th percentile | $44,490 | |
| **Median** | **$59,280** | **$28.50** |
| Average (mean) | $63,630 | $30.59 |
| 75th percentile | $77,250 | |
| Top 10% earned more than | $101,040 | |

*Source: BLS OEWS, May 2025, occupation 33-2011, from the BLS national data file downloaded October 27, 2026.*

These are straight-time wages. Firefighters usually work 24-hour shifts, according to BLS, and many departments add overtime, holiday pay and allowances that BLS does not count.

*Source: BLS OEWS, May 2025, national estimates.*

Promotion is where the money is. The median fire officer earned about $34,000 more than the median firefighter in May 2025. BLS says the usual ladder runs from firefighter to engineer, lieutenant, captain, battalion chief, assistant chief, deputy chief and chief, and that many departments want a bachelor's degree for ranks above battalion chief.

## Where firefighters work

Most paid firefighters, 302,660 of the 345,990 BLS counted, work for local governments, at a median of $59,850. State governments employ 13,240 at $64,980 and the federal government 7,430 at $63,270. Another 18,660 work for private support-service firms, at a median of $37,520.

## Job outlook for firefighters

BLS projects employment of firefighters to grow 4% from 2025 to 2035, from 355,300 to 368,400 jobs, about as fast as the average for all occupations. It expects about 26,800 openings a year, mostly to replace firefighters who retire or leave.

## How to become a firefighter

BLS says firefighters typically need a high school diploma and emergency medical training, and most go through a fire academy. Applicants usually must be at least 18, hold a driver's license, and pass medical, drug and fitness tests. Departments often require EMT certification.

## How I checked these numbers

Every state, metro and national figure comes from the BLS May 2025 Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics data files, which I downloaded on October 27, 2026, for occupation 33-2011. They count paid firefighters' straight-time pay without overtime. FDNY and LAFD scales come from each department's recruiting site.

# Sam Altman Net Worth 2026: A Billionaire With No OpenAI Shares

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked October 25, 2026**

Forbes estimated Sam Altman's net worth at about $3.2 billion on October 25, 2026. Almost none of it comes from OpenAI, the $852 billion company he runs, because he holds no direct OpenAI equity. His wealth is in venture investments. A court exhibit in Elon Musk's lawsuit against OpenAI valued his stake in fusion company Helion at $1.7 billion and his Stripe stake at $633 million as of December 31, 2025.

Forbes' figure has moved a lot as his private stakes were revalued. Reuters, citing Forbes, put him at $4 billion on May 13, 2026, the week his holdings were shown in court. All of these are estimates; most of what he owns is in private companies with no daily share price.

## Does Sam Altman own part of OpenAI?

Not directly. Forbes says he "has no equity in OpenAI," and Reuters reported during the May 2026 trial that he holds no direct OpenAI equity. In December 2024, OpenAI told TechCrunch he had once held a negligible indirect interest through a Sequoia fund, which he no longer has.

That is unusual for a CEO. OpenAI closed a $122 billion funding round at an $852 billion valuation on March 31, 2026, Bloomberg Law reported. Even a 1% stake would be worth about $8.5 billion, more than twice his estimated fortune.

## Where Sam Altman's money comes from

In May 2026, Musk's lawyers showed the jury a list of Altman's stakes in nine companies that had done business with OpenAI, with values as of December 31, 2025. Reuters reported the figures:

| Company | What it does | Altman's stake |
|---|---|---|
| Helion Energy | Nuclear fusion power | $1.7 billion |
| Stripe | Online payments | $633 million |
| Retro Biosciences | Longevity drugs | $258 million |
| Cerebras | AI chips | About $3.2 million |
| Lattice (Degree), Humane, Software Applications, Formation Bio | Software and health | Not given |

The stakes in those nine companies total more than $2 billion. Altman testified that he owns roughly one-third of Helion and left its board in March 2026, GeekWire reported. Reuters reported that he had sold his Reddit stake, worth more than $600 million when Reddit went public in 2024, by the end of 2025. A jury ruled against Musk on May 18, 2026, finding his claims were filed too late, Al Jazeera reported.

## Is Sam Altman a billionaire?

Yes, by Forbes' count. He was No. 2,692 on Forbes' 2024 billionaires list, No. 2,233 in 2025 and No. 1,251 in 2026, according to his Forbes profile, which also lists his source of wealth as investments, not OpenAI.

## How I checked these numbers

I used Forbes for every net worth figure, with its date, and Reuters' reporting of the May 2026 court exhibit for the individual stakes. Those stake values are as of December 31, 2025, and private company values can change a lot from there.

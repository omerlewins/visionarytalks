# Databricks Stock: Can You Buy It, IPO Date and the $190 Billion Valuation

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked November 2, 2026**

No, you cannot buy Databricks stock on a public exchange yet. Databricks is private and has not filed for an IPO. On August 13, 2026 it closed a $5 billion round at a $190 billion valuation, led by Coatue with Blackstone, MGX, T. Rowe Price and Sixth Street Growth, and said its revenue run-rate had passed $7 billion, growing more than 80% a year. CEO Ali Ghodsi says it intends to go public but sees too much distraction in the public market right now.

Databricks sells a data and AI platform that companies use to store, clean and analyze data and to build AI applications on top of it. It was founded in 2013 by the creators of Apache Spark at UC Berkeley and is based in San Francisco.

## When is the Databricks IPO?

There is no date. Ghodsi told CNBC in August 2026 that Databricks is "not just a company that wants to stay in the private" market but sees "too much distraction in the public market" right now, and that he wants to focus on AI products. Many analysts expect a listing in 2027 or later, but that is a forecast, not a plan the company has announced.

Watch SEC EDGAR for a confidential or public S-1. Large companies usually file confidentially first and publish the S-1 a few weeks before trading starts.

## How to buy Databricks stock before an IPO

1. **Secondary marketplaces.** Accredited investors can look for shares on platforms such as Forge Global, but Databricks must usually approve transfers and prices are set privately.
2. **Funds that may hold it.** T. Rowe Price and Fidelity both invested in Databricks rounds, and some of their mutual funds can hold private shares alongside public ones. Check a fund's latest holdings report before buying it for Databricks exposure.
3. **Wait for the IPO.** Once Databricks lists, any brokerage account can buy it.

None of this is investment advice. Private shares are hard to sell and their prices can move sharply at an IPO.

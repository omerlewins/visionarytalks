# Who Owns Whataburger? BDT & MSD Partners and the Dobson Family

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked October 30, 2026**

Whataburger is majority owned by BDT & MSD Partners, the Chicago investment firm co-led by Byron Trott and backed by Michael Dell's family office. BDT Capital Partners bought control from the founding Dobson family in June 2019, and the family kept a minority stake. The price was not disclosed. Whataburger stayed private and headquartered in San Antonio, with about $4.3 billion in 2025 U.S. sales across 1,161 restaurants.

So Whataburger is no longer a family company, but it is not owned by a burger conglomerate either. It is a private, standalone chain inside an investment firm's portfolio.

*Sources: Dallas Morning News and San Antonio Report, June 14, 2019; Whataburger on Wikipedia; QSR 50 2026.*

BDT Capital merged with MSD Partners, the investment arm tied to Michael Dell's family office, in January 2023, so the Whataburger stake now sits with the combined BDT & MSD Partners. Byron Trott, a former Goldman Sachs banker known as an adviser to Warren Buffett, founded BDT and is co-CEO of the merged firm.

## How much did BDT pay for Whataburger?

Neither side disclosed the price. Before the deal, Reuters reported that the chain could fetch a valuation of more than $6 billion. A Bloomberg report reprinted in the Arkansas Democrat-Gazette in October 2019 said the BDT deal valued Whataburger at $4.7 billion. Treat both as reported figures, since no filing confirms them.

The Dobsons hired Morgan Stanley to explore options in May 2019, and the sale to BDT was announced on June 14, 2019. The company said the headquarters would stay in San Antonio, which was the main worry for many Texans at the time.

## Is Whataburger publicly traded?

No. Whataburger is private, so there is no Whataburger stock to buy. The closest thing to public financial information is its debt: Bloomberg reported in 2024 that the company was refinancing a leveraged loan of about $2.7 billion.

## Is Whataburger still owned by the Dobson family?

Partly. The family sold majority control to BDT in 2019 but kept a minority stake, and that stake has not been reported as sold since. Its size has never been published, so figures you may see online, such as 10% to 15%, are estimates rather than disclosed numbers.

# Who Owns Dave's Hot Chicken? Roark Capital and the Four Founders

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked November 2, 2026**

Dave's Hot Chicken is owned by Roark Capital, the Atlanta private equity firm that also owns Subway, Arby's and Buffalo Wild Wings. Roark bought the chain in a deal valued at about $1 billion, announced on June 2, 2025. The four founders, Dave Kopushyan, Arman Oganesyan and brothers Tommy and Gary Rubenyan, stayed on to run food and operations. Jim Bitticks has been CEO since January 2026.

That makes Dave's one of the fastest jumps from parking lot to billion-dollar sale in fast food: eight years from a $900 pop-up in East Hollywood to a Roark portfolio company.

Roark did not publish the terms. The $1 billion figure comes from the AP and earlier reporting by Reuters, which first described the signed deal in February 2025, and some reports describe Roark's purchase as a majority stake. Treat the price as reported rather than disclosed.

## Does Drake own Dave's Hot Chicken?

He was an investor before the sale, not an owner of the company. Drake joined an investor group that also included Samuel L. Jackson, Maria Shriver, Michael Strahan and Tom Werner. Reports on the Roark deal did not say whether any celebrity investors kept a stake afterward.

## Who founded Dave's Hot Chicken?

Four childhood friends from Los Angeles: Dave Kopushyan, a chef who gave the chain its name, Arman Oganesyan, and brothers Tommy and Gary Rubenyan. They opened a pop-up stand in an East Hollywood parking lot on May 2, 2017, with about $900. In 2019, Wetzel's Pretzels co-founder Bill Phelps and film producer John Davis invested, Phelps became CEO, and the chain started franchising.

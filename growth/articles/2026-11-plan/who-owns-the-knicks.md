# Who Owns the Knicks? James Dolan and MSG Knickerbockers Corp.

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked October 30, 2026**

The New York Knicks are owned by MSG Knickerbockers Corp., a public company that was called Madison Square Garden Sports until it spun off the Rangers on October 26, 2026. The Dolan family controls it through Class B shares that carry 70.7% of the voting power, with James Dolan as executive chairman and CEO. Sportico valued the Knicks at $12.8 billion in October 2026, second in the NBA.

The Knicks have never had an individual owner. Since the team was founded in 1946 it has always belonged to whichever company owned Madison Square Garden, and since 1997 that company has been controlled by the Dolans.

*Source: Madison Square Garden Sports Corp. 2026 proxy statement, ownership as of October 15, 2026. "Share of all shares" is my calculation from the 19,589,501 Class A and 4,529,517 Class B shares outstanding that day.*

Each Class B share has ten votes and each Class A share has one, and Class B holders elect nine of the twelve directors. That is why the family controls the Knicks with about a fifth of the equity. The Rangers spin-off did not change Knicks shareholdings: holders kept their shares and received one MSG Rangers share for every two they owned.

## What changed in October 2026?

On February 18, 2026 the MSG Sports board approved a plan to explore splitting the Knicks and Rangers into two public companies, and said the move was not tied to selling either team. The split was completed on October 26, 2026, when the company distributed all MSG Rangers shares to its stockholders and renamed itself MSG Knickerbockers Corp., according to its October 27 SEC filing.

The Knicks company now holds the Knicks and the Westchester Knicks of the G League. MSG Rangers Corp. holds the Rangers and the Hartford Wolf Pack. The Dolan family controls both, and James Dolan stays executive chairman and CEO of the Knicks company. He has said he has no interest in selling either team, per Forbes in June 2026.

## How did the Dolans get the Knicks?

Viacom sold Madison Square Garden, the Knicks, the Rangers and the MSG Network to a partnership of ITT and Cablevision for $1.075 billion in a deal agreed in August 1994 and completed in March 1995. Cablevision, run by the Dolan family, bought ITT's half for $650 million in 1997 and took full control. James Dolan became MSG chairman in 1999, when Forbes valued the Knicks at $334 million.

Cablevision spun the Garden and its teams off as a separate public company in 2010, so the Knicks stayed with the Dolans when Altice bought Cablevision in 2016.

## Does James Dolan own the Knicks?

He controls them but does not own them outright. James Dolan personally holds 23.2% of the voting power, and the wider Dolan Family Group holds 70.7%, as of October 15, 2026. Public shareholders own most of the equity. Forbes estimated the Dolan family fortune at $7 billion in June 2026, split among James Dolan and his five siblings' families.

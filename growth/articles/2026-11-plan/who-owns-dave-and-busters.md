# Who Owns Dave & Buster's? Hill Path Capital and the Public Shareholders

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked November 2, 2026**

Dave & Buster's is a public company traded on the Nasdaq as PLAY, so it is owned by its shareholders. The largest is Hill Path Capital, the New York investment firm run by Scott Ross, with 20.47% of the shares as of April 23, 2026. Eminence Capital (8.35%), Patient Capital Management (6.33%) and BlackRock (5.99%) are the other holders above 5%. Darin Harper has been CEO since August 2026.

No founder or family controls the company today. The two founders, Dave Corriveau and Buster Corley, sold out decades ago, and the chain has passed through a retailer, two private equity firms and a stock market listing since.

*Source: Dave & Buster's 2026 proxy statement, as of April 23, 2026, when 34,739,405 shares were outstanding.*

Hill Path is an active investor, not a passive index fund. Its co-founder James Chambers joined the board in December 2020 under a cooperation agreement with the company, and managing partner Scott Ross joined the board on January 30, 2025.
## Is Dave & Buster's publicly traded?

Yes. Dave & Buster's Entertainment, Inc. has traded on the Nasdaq under the ticker PLAY since its October 2014 IPO, which sold 5.88 million shares and raised about $94 million. Anyone can buy the stock, and the company files annual reports and proxy statements with the SEC.

## Who owns Main Event?

Dave & Buster's does. It bought Main Event Entertainment from Ardent Leisure and RedBird Capital Partners for $835 million, announced in April 2022 and completed in June 2022. Main Event now runs as a second brand inside the same public company.

## Who runs Dave & Buster's?

Darin Harper became CEO on August 3, 2026, after Tarun Lal, a former KFC U.S. president who took the job in July 2025, retired to spend more time with his family in India. Harper had been CFO since June 2024 and earlier was CFO of Main Event. Lal stays on as an adviser through at least the end of fiscal 2027. Kevin Sheehan is the independent chair of the board.

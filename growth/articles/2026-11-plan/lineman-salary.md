# Lineman Salary 2026: Power Line Worker Pay in Every State and City

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked October 27, 2026**

Linemen, the electrical power-line installers and repairers, earned a median of $95,320 a year in May 2025, or $45.83 an hour, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics. Hawaii pays the most, with a median of $134,340, and Arizona the least, at $75,420. Linemen at power utilities earned a median $102,550. Telecom linemen earned less, at $74,330. BLS projects 10% job growth from 2025 to 2035.

That is high pay for a job BLS lists as needing only a high school diploma plus long-term training. It is also one of the most dangerous: BLS says linemen work with high-voltage electricity, often at great heights, and some work more than 40 hours a week. The figures here are straight-time pay, so storm and overtime pay come on top.

## How much do linemen make?

BLS counted 131,070 power-line installer and repairer jobs in May 2025. Their pay spread:

|---|---|---|

| 25th percentile | $67,270 | |

| Average (mean) | $91,970 | $44.22 |

*Source: BLS OEWS, May 2025, occupation 49-9051, from the BLS national data file downloaded October 27, 2026.*

Unusually, the median is higher than the average. That points to a long tail of lower-paid workers below a large group of well-paid linemen; the employer table below shows contractor crews earn much less than utility linemen.

## Lineman vs electrician and telecom lineman pay

| Job (BLS title) | Jobs | Median, May 2025 | Average |
|---|---|---|---|
| Powerhouse, substation and relay electrical repairers | 20,720 | $103,020 | $100,760 |
| **Electrical power-line installers and repairers** | **131,070** | **$95,320** | **$91,970** |
| Telecommunications line installers and repairers | 97,720 | $74,330 | $73,940 |
| Electricians | 757,220 | $63,190 | $71,490 |

*Source: BLS OEWS, May 2025, national estimates.*

A power lineman at the median earns about $32,000 more than an electrician and about $21,000 more than a telecom lineman, who works on phone, cable and fiber lines. BLS does not split out apprentice, journeyman or foreman pay; the lowest 10% figure, $51,470, is the closest guide to apprentice wages. The employer matters as much as the state. Linemen at power utilities earned about $26,500 more at the median than those at line construction contractors. The best-paid group works at natural gas distribution companies, at $137,360, though it is small.

## Job outlook for linemen

BLS projects employment of electrical power-line installers and repairers to grow 10% from 2025 to 2035, from 131,900 to 145,500 jobs, much faster than the average for all occupations. It expects about 10,900 openings a year, from new jobs and from linemen who retire or leave.

## How to become a lineman

Linemen typically need a high school diploma, then technical instruction and long-term on-the-job training, according to BLS. Most complete apprenticeships or employer programs lasting up to 3 years, sometimes run jointly with the union. Entry can require a year of high school algebra, an aptitude test, a drug screen and a fitness test.

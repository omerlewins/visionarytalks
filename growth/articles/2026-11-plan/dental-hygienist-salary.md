# Dental Hygienist Salary 2026: Pay in Every State, City and Setting

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked October 27, 2026**

Dental hygienists in the US earned a median of $98,100 a year in May 2025, or $47.16 an hour, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics. Alaska pays the most among states, with a median of $132,690, and Alabama the least, at $61,160. The best-paid metro area is San Francisco, at $146,760. BLS projects 8% job growth from 2025 to 2035.

That puts hygienists close to six figures with an associate's degree, which is the usual entry route. The catch is hours: BLS says many hygienists work part time, and its annual figures assume a full-time year, so the hourly rate is often the better guide to what a hygienist actually takes home.

## How much do dental hygienists make?

BLS counted 222,740 dental hygienist jobs in May 2025. Their pay spread:

*Source: BLS OEWS, May 2025, occupation 29-1292, from the BLS national data file downloaded October 27, 2026.*

The range is narrow for a health job. Even the lowest-paid 10% earned close to $75,000 on a full-time basis, and the median works out to about $8,175 a month before tax.

## Dental hygienist vs dentist and dental assistant pay

| Job (BLS title) | Jobs | Median, May 2025 | Average |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dentists, all other specialists | 5,230 | $224,990 | $247,930 |
| Dentists, general | 124,390 | $170,950 | $191,350 |
| **Dental hygienists** | **222,740** | **$98,100** | **$98,990** |
| Dental assistants | 387,790 | $48,070 | $50,200 |

*Source: BLS OEWS, May 2025, national estimates.*

A hygienist at the median earns about twice as much as a dental assistant and a little under 60% of what a general dentist earns. The difference with assistants comes down to licensing: every state requires hygienists to be licensed, which usually means an accredited dental hygiene program plus written and clinical exams, according to BLS.

About 95% of hygienist jobs are in dentists' offices. Temp and staffing agencies pay the highest median of the larger settings, $102,340, which fits a job where many hygienists fill in for several practices. Government and university jobs pay less but usually come with fuller benefits.

## Job outlook for dental hygienists

BLS projects employment of dental hygienists to grow 8% from 2025 to 2035, from 225,300 to 243,200 jobs, much faster than the average for all occupations. It expects about 15,000 openings a year, most of them to replace hygienists who retire or change careers. BLS ties part of the growth to state laws that increasingly let hygienists work at the top of their training.

## How to become a dental hygienist

BLS says hygienists typically need an associate's degree in dental hygiene, and programs usually take 3 years. Every state requires a license, and most require a degree from an accredited program plus written and clinical exams. Bachelor's and master's degrees are mainly needed for research, teaching or public health roles.

## How I checked these numbers

The outlook and training figures come from the BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook. BLS annual wages are full-time equivalents and do not include benefits.

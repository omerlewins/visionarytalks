# Palmer Luckey Net Worth 2026: Oculus Sale to Anduril Stake

*By Daniel Mercer. Figures checked October 16, 2026.*

Palmer Luckey is worth about **$6 billion**, according to Forbes' real-time billionaires tracker as of October 15, 2026, which ranks him No. 679 in the world. Almost all of that fortune now comes from his stake in Anduril Industries, the defense company he cofounded in 2017 and which investors valued at **$61 billion** in a $5 billion Series H round announced on May 13, 2026. The Oculus sale to Facebook in 2014 made him rich; Anduril made him a multibillionaire.

I pulled every dated Forbes estimate I could open, from 2016 to this week, and lined each one up against the Anduril funding round closest to it. The pattern is simple: his net worth moves when Anduril raises money at a higher price, and almost never in between.

That is what owning one large private stake looks like on paper.

## How did Palmer Luckey become a billionaire?

He did it in two steps.

**Step one: Oculus.** Luckey founded Oculus VR in 2012 (per Forbes' Dec. 29, 2025 40 Under 40 list) and sold it to Facebook in 2014. I read Facebook's March 25, 2014 press release and the matching Form 8-K filed with the SEC. The terms were:

| Oculus deal term | Figure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Announced | March 25, 2014 | Facebook press release |
| Headline value | About $2 billion | Facebook press release |
| Cash | $400 million | Facebook press release; 8-K |
| Stock | 23,071,377 Facebook Class B shares, valued at $1.6 billion | 8-K |
| Share price used | $69.35, the 20 trading day average before March 21, 2014 | 8-K |
| Earnout | Up to $300 million more in cash and stock if milestones were met | Facebook press release |
| Earnout detail | Up to $60 million in cash and 3,460,706 shares | 8-K |
| Expected close | Second quarter of 2014 | Facebook press release; 8-K |

Luckey's personal share of that $2 billion has never been disclosed in a filing I could find. The first dated Forbes figure I could open is $700 million, on March 14, 2016, when he was 23. He left Facebook in 2016, according to his Forbes profile.

**Step two: Anduril.** He cofounded Anduril Industries in 2017 with Trae Stephens, Brian Schimpf, Matt Grimm and Joseph Chen. Forbes was calling him a billionaire by June 2022, when Anduril was valued at $8 billion, and put a number on it ($2.3 billion) in July 2024. Every later jump in his net worth tracks an Anduril round. For the full ownership picture, see our breakdown of [who owns Anduril](/who-owns-anduril/).

### How old was Palmer Luckey when he sold Oculus?

He was **21**. Luckey was born on September 19, 1992 (per Wikipedia's biography), and Facebook announced the deal on March 25, 2014. Forbes' own ages agree: it listed him at 23 in March 2016, at 33 on the Forbes 400 measured September 4, 2026, and at 34 in October 2026, just after a mid September birthday. That makes him one of the youngest founders ever to sell a company for $2 billion. See where he sits on our list of the [youngest billionaires](/youngest-billionaires/).

## What does Anduril make?

Anduril builds autonomous military hardware and the software that ties it together. In plain terms it sells weapons and surveillance systems that can operate with little or no human piloting, mainly to the U.S. Department of Defense and allied governments.

| Product or project | What it is | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Lattice | AI software that pulls in data from satellites, drones, radar and cameras and coordinates "smart weapons" | CBS News, 60 Minutes (May 2025) |
| Fury | Unmanned fighter jet built to fly alongside piloted aircraft, a Collaborative Combat Aircraft candidate for the U.S. Air Force | CBS News, 60 Minutes (May 2025) |
| Barracuda-500M | Surface launched Barracuda-500M, under a production agreement with the Department of War announced May 13, 2026 | Anduril newsroom |
| Solid rocket motors | Expanded production at its Mississippi facility, backed by an additional $43.7 million in Defense Production Act Title III funding (Feb. 19, 2026) | Anduril newsroom |
| Arsenal-1 | Planned factory of more than 5 million square feet for autonomous systems production, announced with the Series F | Anduril, Aug. 7, 2024 |

On the money side, TechCrunch reported on May 13, 2026 that Anduril's revenue **doubled in 2025 to $2.2 billion**, citing a blog post by CEO Brian Schimpf. At the Series H price of $61 billion, investors paid roughly 28 times that 2025 revenue. Anduril is private, so there are no audited public financials; the $2.2 billion is the company's own figure as reported.

## How much of Anduril does Palmer Luckey own?

**His exact stake is not public.** Anduril is a private company and does not disclose its cap table, and none of the Forbes articles I opened (May 2026, June 2025, December 2025, September 2026) states a percentage for Luckey. Forbes says his fortune is "mainly thanks to Anduril" (Sept. 15, 2026), and the way his number doubled with Anduril's valuation in May 2026 confirms the stake is his dominant asset.

What Forbes has published is the stake of two of his cofounders, which gives useful context:

| Cofounder | Reported stake | Forbes net worth | Date | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Palmer Luckey | Not disclosed | $5 billion | May 15, 2026 | Forbes |
| Trae Stephens (executive chairman) | More than 3% | Became a billionaire | June 10, 2025 | Forbes |
| Trae Stephens | Not stated | $1.8 billion | May 15, 2026 | Forbes |
| Brian Schimpf (CEO) | Over 2% | $1.5 billion | May 15, 2026 | Forbes |

Luckey's net worth is more than three times Stephens' and Schimpf's, which tells you he holds the largest founder stake of the three. I am not going to convert that into a percentage, because Forbes applies private company discounts and counts other assets (cash from Oculus, real estate) that it does not itemize. Any site quoting a precise "Luckey owns X% of Anduril" figure is guessing unless it cites a filing.

Luckey is also reportedly working on a banking startup called Erebor, according to Forbes' Dec. 29, 2025 list. No valuation or stake for him in Erebor appears in the sources I opened, so it is not counted here. For another young founder whose fortune is mostly private stakes, see our [Daniel Gross net worth](/daniel-gross-net-worth/) breakdown.

## Is Palmer Luckey married?

Yes. Luckey is married to Nicole Edelmann, his partner since they were 15, according to Tablet Magazine's 2024 profile and his Wikipedia biography (which dates the wedding to 2019); note that Forbes' profile still lists his marital status as "Single" as of October 15, 2026.

## Why does Palmer Luckey wear Hawaiian shirts?

In his own words, quoted by OfficeChai in December 2025 from an Anduril post on X, his father was a car salesman who wore Hawaiian shirts on his one day off, so "my entire wardrobe through my teenage years was all my dad's Hawaiian shirts," and he keeps wearing them to "remember where I come from."

## Where does Luckey rank among billionaires?

On the 2026 Forbes 400 (net worths as of September 4, 2026), Luckey debuted at No. 287 with $5.9 billion. He was one of 34 newcomers and the second youngest member of the list. The cutoff to make the 2026 Forbes 400 was a record $4.4 billion, so his May 2026 jump from $3.5 billion to $5 billion is what carried him over the line. He lives in Newport Beach, which puts him in California's billionaire count; see [how many billionaires live in each state](/how-many-billionaires-by-state/).

# Who Owns Liquid Death? Mike Cessario, Investors and the $1.4B Valuation

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked October 25, 2026**

Liquid Death is made by Supplying Demand, Inc., a private Los Angeles company led by founder and CEO Mike Cessario. Its shareholders are Cessario, early staff and venture and strategic investors including Science Inc. and Live Nation. Its last priced round, in March 2024, valued it at $1.4 billion.

Supplying Demand, Inc. is the legal name. Liquid Death's own website terms say the site and the business are "operated by Supplying Demand, Inc dba Liquid Death." No big drinks company owns it. Coca-Cola, PepsiCo and Keurig Dr Pepper have no announced stake.

The Form D notices Supplying Demand, Inc. filed with the SEC from May 2019 to March 2024 add up to about $239 million of stock and notes sold, counting each round once. I have not found a company-confirmed total since.

One investor publishes what its stake is worth. SuRo Capital, now called Neostellar Capital Corp., is a listed investment fund. Its 10-Q for June 30, 2026 shows 776,747 Series F-1 preferred shares of Supplying Demand, Inc. bought on January 18, 2024 for about $10.0 million and still valued at about $10.0 million, plus a $250,000 convertible note issued July 29, 2025. That is one fund's estimate, not a new company valuation, but it shows at least one holder has not marked the stock down.

## Is Liquid Death a public company?

No. Liquid Death has not filed to go public, so you cannot buy its shares on a stock exchange. CNN reported in October 2022 that an investor said the company was "beginning to lay the groundwork for the IPO path in case it makes sense." As of October 25, 2026, the only SEC filings under Supplying Demand, Inc. are Form D notices for private fundraising, the last on March 15, 2024. There is no IPO registration. Shares sometimes change hands on private secondary markets, but those prices are not official valuations.

## Who founded Liquid Death?

Mike Cessario, a former creative director at Netflix, according to SoCalTech's 2019 report on the seed round. He launched the brand in 2019 with backing from Science Inc., the Santa Monica startup studio behind Dollar Shave Club. Science's Michael Jones, the former CEO of MySpace, joined Liquid Death's board at the seed round. Cessario is still CEO.

In October 2025, Liquid Death hired former PepsiCo and Sabra executive Ricky Khetarpaul as CFO, as it prepared to launch energy drinks, CFO Dive reported.

## Mike Cessario net worth

There is no reliable figure, and I will not invent one. Cessario's stake has never been disclosed, and the company publishes no cap table. Sites that print a precise number are guessing at both his percentage and the company's value. What can be said: if he still owned even a few percent of a company last valued at $1.4 billion in March 2024, his paper stake would be worth tens of millions of dollars. It is illiquid until Liquid Death is sold or goes public.

## Where does Liquid Death water come from?

Today, from the Blue Ridge Mountains in Bland, Virginia. That is the source Liquid Death names on its own water quality reports page, checked October 25, 2026. When the brand launched, it sourced water from the Austrian Alps, SoCalTech reported in 2019. It has since moved its mountain water source to the US.

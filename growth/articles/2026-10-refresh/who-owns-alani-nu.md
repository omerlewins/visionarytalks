# Who Owns Alani Nu? Celsius Bought It for $1.8B (2025)

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked October 25, 2026**

Alani Nu is owned by Celsius Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: CELH), which completed its purchase on April 1, 2025. Celsius announced the deal at $1.8 billion, paid in cash and Celsius stock. PepsiCo distributes Alani Nu in the US and Canada but does not own the brand.

The company Celsius bought is Alani Nutrition LLC of Louisville, Kentucky, now a wholly owned subsidiary of Celsius. Because Celsius is listed on Nasdaq, Alani Nu's ultimate owners are Celsius shareholders. For who controls Celsius itself, see [who owns Celsius](/who-owns-celsius/).

## The deal, from Celsius's filings

| Term | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Announced | February 20, 2025 | Celsius press release, Form 8-K |
| Headline price | $1.8 billion, including about $150 million of tax assets, for a net price of $1.65 billion | Celsius press release |
| Completed | April 1, 2025 | Celsius Form 8-K, April 1, 2025 |
| Cash at closing | $1,275.0 million, subject to adjustment | Celsius 2025 10-K |
| Celsius stock | 22,451,224 shares of Celsius common stock | Celsius Form 8-K and 10-K |
| Earn-out | Up to $25 million more in cash if Alani Nu hit a 2025 net sales target. Celsius said the target was met and expected to pay it in the first quarter of 2026 | Celsius 2025 10-K |
| How the cash was raised | A $900 million term loan drawn at closing, plus cash on hand | Celsius 2025 10-K |
| Who was paid | Alani Holdings, LLC, a holding company the sellers set up for the deal | Celsius Form 8-K |
| Sellers | Co-founders Katy Schneider (Katy Hearn) and Haydn Schneider, their trusts, Max Clemons and Trey Steiger. Congo Brands Holding Company LLC was also a party | Celsius Form 8-K |

In its accounts, Celsius recorded total purchase consideration of $2.06 billion, higher than the $1.8 billion headline because it values the shares at the price on the closing day and adds adjustments. It booked $734.4 million of that as goodwill.

## How big is Alani Nu inside Celsius?

Big. Alani Nu brought in 39.8% of Celsius's consolidated revenue in 2025, according to the 2025 10-K, even though Celsius only owned it from April 1. Celsius's total 2025 revenue was $2.52 billion, so Alani Nu's share works out to about $1.0 billion for those nine months. When Celsius announced the deal, it said the price was less than three times Alani Nu's 2024 net revenue.

## Is Alani Nu Coke or Pepsi?

Neither owns it, but Pepsi is closely involved. Since August 28, 2025, PepsiCo has been the primary distributor of Alani Nu and Rockstar in the US and Canada, under agreements Celsius signed when it bought Rockstar from PepsiCo. PepsiCo agreed to pay up to $275 million to cover fees for ending Alani Nu's old distribution contracts.

PepsiCo also owns a large stake in Celsius through preferred shares and has two directors on Celsius's board, the 10-K says. So Pepsi has influence over the company that owns Alani Nu, but it does not own Alani Nu, and Celsius remains a separate listed company. Coca-Cola has no announced stake in Alani Nu or Celsius.

## What happened to the founders after the sale?

Katy Schneider, better known as fitness creator Katy Hearn, and her husband Haydn Schneider founded Alani Nu in 2018. They sold their interests to Celsius in the deal. In Celsius's announcement, Katy Schneider said she had "full confidence" Celsius was the right partner for the brand's next chapter. Celsius's filings do not name her in any executive role at Celsius, and I have found no filing that says how much each founder received.

The Celsius stock went to Alani Holdings, LLC, whose sole members are Max Clemons and Trey Steiger, the founders of Louisville-based Congo Brands, which built and ran Alani Nu's operations. That makes them one of Celsius's largest shareholders:

| Holder | Celsius shares | Share of Celsius | As of | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alani Holdings, LLC (Max Clemons and Trey Steiger) | 22,451,224 | 8.74% | April 1, 2026 | Celsius 2026 proxy |

Celsius registered all 22,451,224 shares for resale on June 20, 2025, so Alani Holdings is free to sell them. As of the 2026 proxy, it still held all of them.

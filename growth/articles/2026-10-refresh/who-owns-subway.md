# Who Owns Subway? Roark Capital Bought It for Up to $9.55B (2024)

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked October 25, 2026**

Subway is owned by Roark Capital, an Atlanta private equity firm that completed its purchase on April 30, 2024. Reuters reported the deal at up to $9.55 billion including debt. The restaurants themselves, nearly 37,000 of them, are owned and run by franchisees.

That is two layers of ownership, and both answers are right. Roark owns the company and the brand. Franchisees own almost every restaurant and pay Subway fees to use it. Roark's own portfolio page, checked October 25, 2026, says Subway has "nearly 37,000 restaurants" in more than 100 countries and that they "are owned and operated by Subway franchisees."

Roark kept Subway as a separate company inside its portfolio. In July 2025 Subway named Jonathan Fitzpatrick, a former Burger King executive who had run Roark-backed Driven Brands, as CEO. He succeeded John Chidsey, who retired at the end of 2024.

## What else does Roark own?

Roark is one of the biggest owners of restaurant chains in the US. These are the food brands its portfolio page lists, checked October 25, 2026:

| Roark company | Brands |
|---|---|
| Inspire Brands | Arby's, Baskin-Robbins, Buffalo Wild Wings, Dunkin', Jimmy John's, SONIC |
| GoTo Foods | Auntie Anne's, Carvel, Cinnabon, Jamba, McAlister's, Moe's Southwest Grill, Schlotzsky's |
| Subway | Subway |
| CKE Restaurants | Carl's Jr., Hardee's |

That puts Subway and Jimmy John's, two sandwich chains, under the same owner, which drew attention while the deal waited for regulatory approval. It closed in April 2024.

## Who owned Subway before Roark?

The two founding families. In 1965, 17-year-old Fred DeLuca asked family friend Dr. Peter Buck, a nuclear physicist, how to pay for college. Buck suggested a sandwich shop and put in $1,000, and that summer they opened Pete's Super Submarines in Bridgeport, Connecticut, according to Subway's own history page. They began franchising in 1974, after opening 16 restaurants in Connecticut.

Each side owned half until the 2024 sale. DeLuca died in 2015 and left his half to his family. Buck died in 2021 and left his half to a charity run by his sons, CNBC reported when the sale was announced. Those two owners, the DeLuca family and the Peter Buck foundation, were the sellers.

## Is Subway bigger than McDonald's?

Not by restaurant count, and almost certainly not by sales.

| Measure | Subway | McDonald's | Dates and sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Restaurants worldwide | Nearly 37,000 | 45,356 | Roark, checked Oct 25, 2026; McDonald's 10-K, end of 2025 |
| Share franchised | Almost all, per Roark and Subway | About 95% | Same |
| Sales | Not published | $139.4 billion systemwide in 2025 | McDonald's 10-K |

Subway's US count peaked at 27,100 in 2015 and fell to about 20,600 by the end of 2022, CNBC reported, citing Subway's franchise disclosure documents. Because Subway is private, it does not publish total sales, so there is no like-for-like sales comparison.

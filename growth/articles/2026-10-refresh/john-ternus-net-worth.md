# John Ternus Net Worth 2026: Apple CEO Pay and Shares

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked October 25, 2026**

John Ternus, Apple's CEO since September 1, 2026, owned 59,567 Apple shares after his October 2, 2026 sales, worth about $20.7 million at the October 23 close. He also holds unvested awards worth about $144 million at target. His full net worth is not public; the filings support at least about $29 million.

Ternus, 50, joined Apple in 2001 and ran hardware engineering from 2021. Apple's board appointed him CEO on April 17, 2026, and Tim Cook became Executive Chair on the same day Ternus took over, according to Apple's 8-K of April 20, 2026. Because he only became a reporting insider on September 1, 2026, his SEC filings start then. Everything below comes from those filings, priced at Apple's closing price of $347.73 on October 23, 2026.

So the part of his Apple wealth that is documented and already his is about **$29 million**: $20.7 million of stock plus about $8.5 million of pre-tax sale proceeds. That is a floor, not an estimate of everything he has. Ternus has been vesting Apple stock for years. His Form 3 shows 41,629 shares vesting on April 1, 2026 alone, more than the 34,155 shares he held on September 1, 2026, so he had already sold or had shares withheld before becoming CEO. What happened to those shares before he became CEO, and what else he owns, is not public.

The unvested awards add about **$144 million** at target and today's price. They are not his yet. He loses them if he leaves before they vest, and the performance awards can pay anything from nothing to twice the target.

## His first big vesting as CEO

On October 1, 2026, a performance award granted in October 2023 paid out. Its target was 58,408 shares. Apple's total shareholder return over fiscal 2024 to 2026 was 90.67%, which ranked 115th of 480 S&P 500 companies, the 76th percentile, so 99,878 shares vested, about 171% of target.

Apple withheld 49,054 of those shares, worth about $16.2 million at $330.32, to cover taxes. On October 2, Ternus sold 25,412 more at between about $331 and $334 under a Rule 10b5-1 trading plan he adopted on May 21, 2026. That left him the 59,567 shares above.

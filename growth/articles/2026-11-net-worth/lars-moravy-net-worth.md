# Lars Moravy Net Worth 2026: Tesla's Engineering Chief Has No SEC Footprint

## The investigation: zero filings in 16 years

I started where every profile in this series starts, with SEC EDGAR. Tesla's executive officers, directors and 10% owners must report every stock trade on a Form 4 within two business days, and Tesla's proxy reports the pay of its top executives each year.

A full-text search of Tesla's filings for "Moravy" returns no results. That covers Form 4s, proxies, annual reports and the investor materials Tesla files as 8-Ks. It means three things:

1. **He is not a Section 16 officer.** Tesla treats only a handful of top executives as reporting officers. Vice presidents who run large teams, including Moravy, can sit outside that group.
2. **His pay is private.** Tesla's proxy covers only its named executive officers, so his salary and stock awards have never been published.
3. **His trades are private.** If he has received, exercised or sold Tesla stock, no filing shows it.

This is the same pattern I found for Tesla's chief designer, Franz von Holzhausen, who has led design since 2008 and has also never filed a Form 4. The difference is that von Holzhausen at least appears by name in a 2009 investor agreement. Moravy does not appear anywhere.

## What could he be worth?

The ingredients point to real wealth, but the amounts are unknown.

- **Timing.** He joined Tesla in 2010, the year of its June IPO at $17 a share, which is $1.13 after the 2020 and 2022 stock splits. Any stock he received that year and kept has risen many times over.
- **Tenure.** He has been at Tesla for about 16 years, and senior Tesla employees usually receive new stock grants every year.
- **Seniority.** He leads one of Tesla's largest engineering groups and represented the company before a Senate committee, which suggests he is among its better-paid employees.
- **The unknowns.** How many shares or options he received, how many he sold, what he paid in tax and what he spent are all private.

Because of those unknowns, I can't responsibly put a number on it. If he kept a meaningful share of his early grants, his Tesla stock alone could be worth tens of millions of dollars or more. Any site that gives a precise figure is guessing, and none that I found shows its math.

## His work at Tesla

In his Senate testimony, Moravy said he oversees "vehicle design, automation, and manufacturing processes" and develops "new systems to ensure quality, reliability, and performance."

In a July 2025 interview with Fortune, he described the company's approach this way: "We take big swings." He said Tesla was "in a big swing moment right now with autonomy, Robotaxis, with Optimus and with Semi."

### The February 2026 Senate testimony

On February 4, 2026, Moravy testified before the Senate Commerce, Science and Transportation Committee, chaired by Ted Cruz. He asked Congress to pass a federal framework for autonomous vehicles and to update safety rules he said "have not kept pace" with electric drivetrains, automated driving and over-the-air software updates.

His testimony included several Tesla figures:

## Who is Lars Moravy?

Moravy told the Senate that he "fell in love with designing and building cars at the age of 16," when he and his brother spent a summer restoring a Volvo that had sat in his grandparents' barn for years. That led him into the car industry and to Honda, where he worked before Tesla, according to Fortune. Professional profiles say he studied mechanical engineering at Northwestern University.

He joined Tesla in 2010, when it was still building the original Roadster and preparing the Model S. "I jumped at the opportunity to build the best cars in the world in an innovative way," he said in his testimony. More than 15 years later, he runs the engineering behind Tesla's vehicles and works closely with chief designer Franz von Holzhausen, Fortune reported.

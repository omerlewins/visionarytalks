## Who is Chuck Robbins?

Charles "Chuck" Robbins grew up in the South, finished high school in Rocky Mount, North Carolina, and studied math at UNC Chapel Hill. He started as a programmer at a bank that later became part of Bank of America, then moved into networking sales at Wellfleet and Ascend before joining Cisco in 1997.

He spent almost two decades in Cisco's sales organization, eventually running its worldwide field operations and building out its partner program. In 2015 Cisco picked him to replace John Chambers, who had run the company for 20 years. As CEO he has pushed Cisco from selling network hardware toward software, subscriptions and security, and in recent years toward networking gear for AI data centers.

He is married with four children, lives in Los Gatos, California, and is a fan of North Carolina Tar Heels basketball, according to Wikipedia.

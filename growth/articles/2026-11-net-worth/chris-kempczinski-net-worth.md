## Who is Chris Kempczinski?

Kempczinski, 57, grew up in Cincinnati, studied at Duke University and earned an MBA from Harvard Business School in 1997, according to Wikipedia. He started in brand management at Procter & Gamble, worked as a consultant at Boston Consulting Group, and held senior roles at PepsiCo and Kraft before McDonald's hired him in 2015 to run strategy.

He ran McDonald's U.S. business and became chief executive in November 2019, succeeding Steve Easterbrook, whom the board removed. He added the chairman title in 2024 and also sits on the board of Procter & Gamble, where he began his career.

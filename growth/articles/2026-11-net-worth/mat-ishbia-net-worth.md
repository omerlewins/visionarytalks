## How the stake is held

Ishbia controls United Wholesale Mortgage through SFS Corp., which holds Class D shares with ten votes each, paired with units in the operating partnership, UWM Holdings LLC. The voting stock of SFS is held by the Mat Ishbia South Dakota Trust, which takes his direction, the proxy says. Formally, Ishbia disclaims a financial interest in SFS's shares, a common estate planning structure; Forbes and others still attribute the stake to him.

Because SFS owns about 79% of the partnership, it received most of UWM's cash distributions. UWM's latest 10-Q shows member distributions to SFS of $148.4 million, $190.9 million and $156.0 million in the first three quarters of 2025, and $129.1 million and $126.8 million in the first two quarters of 2026. In the third quarter of 2026 there were none.

Then the money went the other way. On August 5, 2026, UWM raised $1.65 billion from Oaktree Capital and the Ishbia family company: Oaktree bought $1.5 billion of preferred stock and SFS Group bought $150 million, with warrants, according to UWM's 8-K. UWM followed with a rights offering at $2.00 a share. Ishbia also exercised his own rights for 232,634 shares at $2.00.

SFS now holds 54,863,100 Class A warrants at $6.00 a share and the same number of Class B warrants at $2.00, according to his November 19 Form 4. With UWM at $1.12, all are out of the money.

## Who is Mat Ishbia?

Mathew Randall Ishbia was born on January 6, 1980, and grew up in Birmingham, Michigan, according to Wikipedia. He played as a walk-on point guard at Michigan State from 1999 to 2002 and was on the team that won the 2000 national championship.

He joined his father's mortgage company, became CEO in 2013 and made it the largest wholesale mortgage lender in the U.S. He has given more than $46 million to Michigan State. He divorced in 2023 and has three children.

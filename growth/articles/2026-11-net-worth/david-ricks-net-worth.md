## Who is David Ricks?

Ricks studied at Purdue, earned an MBA at Indiana University and joined Eli Lilly in Indianapolis in 1996 as a business development associate. He spent the next two decades in sales, marketing and country management, running Lilly's businesses in Canada and China and then Lilly USA, the company's largest unit. In 2012 he became president of Lilly Bio-Medicines, and he took over as CEO in January 2017 and as chair that June.

His tenure has been defined by the GLP-1 drugs tirzepatide, sold as Mounjaro for diabetes and Zepbound for weight loss. Demand for them turned Lilly into the world's most valuable drugmaker and drove its market value past $1 trillion, according to BioSpace. Ricks sits on Adobe's board and Purdue's board of trustees, and he is a distinguished Eagle Scout.

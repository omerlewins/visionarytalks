In fiscal 2025 the median Disney employee, a full-time hourly parks worker, earned $56,932. Iger made 805 times that, the proxy says.

These figures cover only 2023 to 2025. Iger ran Disney from 2005 to 2020 as well, and in those years he was regularly among the best-paid CEOs in the United States, which is where most of the fortune Forbes measures came from.

## The Lakers deal

CNBC reported on August 12, 2026 that Walter had sold his majority stake to Kushner and Iger at a $12.5 billion valuation. Walter had bought control from the Buss family less than a year earlier at $10 billion. Iger and Kushner called themselves future "stewards" of the franchise in a joint statement.

As of November 24, 2026, the deal still needed review and approval by the NBA, according to HoopsHype, which said both men were expected at the Lakers' November 23 game against Utah. Approval takes at least 23 of the 30 team owners. USA Today's August report said the process could run into 2027.

Iger knows Kushner well. He was a venture partner at Thrive in 2022, before Disney's board brought him back as CEO, and he rejoined Thrive as an advisor in April 2026, a month after handing Disney to Josh D'Amaro, TechCrunch reported.

## Who is Bob Iger?

Robert Alan Iger was born in New York City on February 10, 1951. He started at ABC in 1974 and rose to run the network, then stayed on when Disney bought ABC's parent in 1996. Disney made him president in 2000 and CEO in 2005.

His first run as CEO is remembered for four acquisitions that reshaped Hollywood: Pixar, Marvel, Lucasfilm and most of 21st Century Fox. He retired in 2020, came back in November 2022 after his successor Bob Chapek was ousted, and handed over to Josh D'Amaro, the former parks chief, on March 18, 2026. (Our [Josh D'Amaro net worth](/josh-damaro-net-worth/) profile covers the new CEO's pay.)

Iger has been married to journalist Willow Bay since 1995. He has four children.

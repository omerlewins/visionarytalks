## Who is Brian Moynihan?

Brian Thomas Moynihan was born on October 9, 1959, in Marietta, Ohio, the sixth of eight children, according to Wikipedia. He studied history at Brown, where he met his wife, Susan, and earned a law degree at Notre Dame before joining a Providence law firm.

He moved into banking at Fleet Boston in 1993 and came to Bank of America through its 2004 merger with FleetBoston. He became CEO of Merrill Lynch after Bank of America bought it in 2008 and took over the whole bank in 2010. He lives in Wellesley, Massachusetts, and often commutes to Bank of America's Charlotte headquarters on company jets.

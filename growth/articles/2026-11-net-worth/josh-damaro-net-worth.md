## Who is Josh D'Amaro?

Joshua D'Amaro was born on February 10, 1971, in Medfield, Massachusetts, sharing a birthday with Bob Iger, 20 years his senior. He started college at Skidmore studying sculpture, then transferred to Georgetown, where he earned a business degree in 1993.

He spent his whole Disney career on the operations side, from selling trips to running Animal Kingdom, Disneyland and Walt Disney World. As chairman of Disney Experiences from 2020 he oversaw the theme parks, cruise line and consumer products.

He is married to Susan D'Amaro and has two children, according to Wikipedia. He also sits on the national board of Make-A-Wish America.

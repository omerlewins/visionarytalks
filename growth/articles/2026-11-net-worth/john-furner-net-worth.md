## Who is John Furner?

Furner was born in 1974 in Jacksonville, Arkansas, and studied marketing at the University of Arkansas's Walton College of Business, according to Wikipedia. He started at Walmart in 1993 as an hourly associate and worked as a store manager, district manager and buyer before senior roles in Walmart China and Sam's Club.

At Sam's Club he closed 63 stores to compete better with Costco, and he ran Walmart U.S., the company's largest business, from 2019 to 2026. He chaired the National Retail Federation's board from 2022 to 2025. He is married with four children.

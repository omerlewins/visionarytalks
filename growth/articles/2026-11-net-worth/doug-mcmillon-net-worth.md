## Why two share counts?

The two filings count different things. Walmart's proxy lists 2,297,783 shares McMillon owned or could own within 60 days of April 10, 2026, and it leaves out unvested performance units and deferred stock units. His Form 4 reports everything he holds.

The gap is mostly deferred pay. Walmart's proxy shows a deferred compensation balance of $353.8 million at January 30, 2026, made up of years of salary, bonuses and stock that he chose to take later, plus $5.3 million of interest and dividend equivalents earned on it in fiscal 2026 alone. The deferred stock part will be taxed as income when it is paid out, so it is worth less to him than its market value.

## Who is Doug McMillon?

Carl Douglas McMillon was born on October 17, 1966, in Memphis, Tennessee, and grew up in Jonesboro and then Bentonville, Arkansas, where Walmart is based, according to Wikipedia. His first Walmart job came at 17, and after business school he started as a buyer of fishing tackle.

As CEO from 2014 he raised hourly wages, pushed Walmart into e-commerce and stopped selling some ammunition after the 2019 El Paso shooting. He lives in Bentonville with his wife, Shelley, and they have two sons.

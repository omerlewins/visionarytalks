## What Axel Dumas owns in his own name

Hermès publishes each executive chairman's direct shareholding. For Dumas, the 2025 registration document lists "11,638 full ownership and 30 in usufruct," all registered shares, at December 31, 2025. The supervisory board requires each executive chairman to hold at least 1,000 shares, and he meets that.

H51 was created in 2011 to lock up the family's control after LVMH built a stake of about 17% through equity swaps. We cover that history in [Who Owns Hermès?](/who-owns-hermes/).

The registration document lists Dumas as a director of H51 and as executive chairman of three other companies, Axam 2, Maia and Mathel, and a director of Krefeld. These look like family holding vehicles, but Hermès does not disclose what each owns or how H51's shares are divided among family members.

## Who is Axel Dumas?

Axel Dumas is a sixth-generation member of the Hermès family and the nephew of Jean-Louis Dumas, who ran Hermès from 1978 to 2006 and turned it into a global luxury house. He studied at Sciences Po in Paris and holds a bachelor's degree in philosophy and a master's in business law, plus Harvard Business School's Advanced Management Program, according to Hermès.

He did not start in the family business. He spent eight years at BNP Paribas, first in China and then in the United States, before joining Hermès in 2003. He ran sales in France, then the jewellery division, then leather goods and saddlery, the business that makes the Birkin and Kelly bags. He became head of operations in 2011, during the fight to keep LVMH out, and executive chairman in June 2013.

Under his leadership Hermès has kept its policy of limited supply and steady price rises. He also sits on the board of Exor, the Agnelli family's investment company, as an independent director.

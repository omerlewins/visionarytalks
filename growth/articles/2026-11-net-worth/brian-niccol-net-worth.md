# Brian Niccol Net Worth 2026: $230 Million in Chipotle Sales, Then Starbucks

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked November 24, 2026**

Brian Niccol's visible wealth is well over $250 million. Form 4 filings show he sold about $230 million of Chipotle stock (gross) between 2019 and April 2024, before he left to run Starbucks. Starbucks then paid him $95.8 million for fiscal 2024, mostly to replace Chipotle awards he gave up, and $31.0 million for fiscal 2025. After a November 19, 2026 filing he holds 497,676 Starbucks shares and share units, worth about $52.9 million at the $106.36 close on November 23, 2026.

That makes Niccol one of the few hired CEOs, not founders, whose wealth runs into the hundreds of millions. He got there by turning around two restaurant chains, and by being paid in stock both times.

### The jet

Niccol lives in Newport Beach, California, and Starbucks is based in Seattle. Starbucks lets him use company aircraft, and since September 2025, on a security study's advice, for all of his travel, including personal trips. He reimbursed Starbucks $0 for personal flights in fiscal 2025, according to the proxy.

## Who is Brian Niccol?

Niccol, 52, studied at Miami University in Ohio before starting in brand management at Procter & Gamble. He moved to Yum Brands in 2005, ran marketing at Pizza Hut, and made his name at Taco Bell, which he led as CEO from 2015.

Chipotle hired him in 2018. Under him the chain added digital ordering and drive-through "Chipotlanes", and its share price rose about eightfold, according to Wikipedia. Starbucks recruited him in 2024 to reverse falling U.S. sales, with a plan he calls "Back to Starbucks."

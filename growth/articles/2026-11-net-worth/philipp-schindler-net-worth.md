## Who is Philipp Schindler?

Schindler grew up in the Gerresheim district of Düsseldorf and studied business at the European Business School, where he was a scholar of Germany's national academic foundation. He started in new media at Bertelsmann in the mid-1990s, then moved to CompuServe and AOL in Germany, becoming a senior vice president at AOL Germany at 28.

He joined Google in Hamburg in 2005 to sell ads in German-speaking countries and the Nordics, took over Central and Northern Europe in 2009 and moved to the US in 2012 to run global sales and operations. When Sundar Pichai became Google's CEO in 2015, Schindler was named chief business officer. He oversees sales for Google and YouTube, customer support and partnerships, and in 2022 led about 29,000 people, roughly 17% of Google's workforce, according to Wikipedia.

He is married with three children and enjoys camping, hydrofoiling and skiing.

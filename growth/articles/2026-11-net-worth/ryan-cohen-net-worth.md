## No salary, and no award

GameStop's proxy says Cohen "does not draw a salary, take a cash bonus, or receive time-vested stock." His only reported pay is the cost of security, $1,760,467 in fiscal 2025 and $268,553 in fiscal 2024.

In January 2026 the board granted him an all-performance option award: 171,537,327 options at $20.66 a share, the closing price on the grant date, in nine tranches that would vest only if GameStop's market value rose as high as $100 billion while it also hit cumulative earnings targets. Press reports valued it at up to $35 billion. It needed shareholder approval at the July 2026 annual meeting.

On June 23, 2026, GameStop said it was withdrawing the proposal at Cohen's request. eBay's board had criticized the award while rejecting Cohen's takeover offer, according to a Financial Times interview GameStop filed with the SEC.

## The eBay pursuit

In May 2026, Cohen offered to buy eBay for about $56 billion in cash and GameStop stock, a company roughly five times GameStop's size. eBay's board rejected it in May, citing the financing, the combined company's debt and Cohen's own incentives, the FT reported in July. By then GameStop had nearly doubled its eBay stake to almost 10%, and Cohen told the FT: "I want to own eBay."

## Who is Ryan Cohen?

Cohen was born in 1986 in Montreal to a teacher mother and a glassware importer father, and never went to college, according to Wikipedia. He started Chewy in 2011 after more than 100 venture firms turned him down, and built it into the largest online pet retailer before selling it to PetSmart in 2017.

He bought into GameStop in 2020, joined its board in January 2021, became chairman that June and took over as CEO in September 2023. Under him GameStop cut costs sharply and returned to profit. He lives in Bal Harbour, Florida.

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked November 17, 2026**

## Tony Xu salary and pay

Xu's annual pay is small because almost all of it was front-loaded into one grant. His base salary has been about $300,000, and reported totals in recent years have been under $500,000, according to compensation trackers that cite DoorDash's proxies.

The real money is his CEO Performance Award, a large grant of restricted stock units made before the 2020 IPO that vests only if DoorDash's stock reaches set price targets. DoorDash's June 2026 10-Q counts it among the awards that would lift the cofounders' combined voting power to 63% if fully vested. Like Elon Musk's Tesla package, it means he earns most of his pay only if shareholders do well first.

## The Instacart deal

On October 29, 2026, DoorDash agreed to buy Instacart for about $17 billion, or $65 per Instacart share, a 26% premium to Instacart's close on October 22. Instacart shareholders can take cash, 0.3372 DoorDash shares, or a mix, with stock capped at 25% of the total. DoorDash plans to pay the cash part from cash on hand and new debt.

"Instacart has spent years learning to do that well for grocers, while DoorDash has built a delivery network and technology that local stores rely on every day," Xu said. The deal needs shareholder and regulatory approval and is expected to close at the end of 2027 or in the first half of 2028.

Because only a quarter of the price is paid in stock, the deal will not dilute Xu much. But DoorDash's shares fell in early November 2026 after Amazon announced a tie-up with Uber Eats, which shows how exposed his fortune is to competition in delivery.

## Who is Tony Xu?

Tony Xu was born in Nanjing and moved to Champaign, Illinois, with his family when he was five. His mother, a doctor in China, worked as a restaurant server in the US, and Xu washed dishes there as a boy, a story he often tells about why DoorDash focuses on small restaurants. He studied industrial engineering at UC Berkeley and worked at McKinsey, eBay and Square before business school at Stanford.

At Stanford in 2013, he and classmates Andy Fang, Stanley Tang and Evan Moore built a simple website listing menus from Palo Alto restaurants and delivered the orders themselves. That became DoorDash, now the largest food delivery company in the US. Fang and Tang each own about 4% of DoorDash, according to Forbes, and have agreed to let Xu vote their shares.

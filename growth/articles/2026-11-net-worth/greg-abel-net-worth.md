## How Greg Abel made his money

### The $870 million energy stake

Abel spent most of his career running the utility business that became Berkshire Hathaway Energy, starting at CalEnergy in 1992. Along the way he acquired a personal stake of about 1% in the company.

In June 2022, Berkshire Hathaway Energy bought that stake back for $870 million, according to Berkshire's quarterly report. Berkshire recorded a $362 million charge to capital on the deal. Analysts noted that he was paid in cash, not in Berkshire stock. Forbes says this payout is the source of most of his fortune.

The price was generous by later standards. When Berkshire bought out the Walter Scott family's remaining stake in 2024 to take full ownership, the implied value of the business was much lower, according to reports at the time.

"Absolute alignment with our shareholders, our partners, our owners is critical," he said. He estimated that this would add up to "hundreds of millions" over his career.

## Who is Greg Abel?

Gregory Edward Abel was born in Edmonton in 1962 and studied commerce at the University of Alberta. His uncle, Sid Abel, is in the Hockey Hall of Fame.

He trained as an accountant at PricewaterhouseCoopers, where CalEnergy was a client, according to Wikipedia. He joined CalEnergy in 1992 and rose with it as it bought other utilities and became MidAmerican Energy. Berkshire took control of MidAmerican in 1999 and 2000, and Abel became its CEO in 2008. Under his leadership it became Berkshire Hathaway Energy, one of the largest utility companies in the US.

In 2018 Buffett put him on Berkshire's board as vice chair responsible for every business except insurance, from BNSF Railway to Dairy Queen. Buffett confirmed in 2021 that Abel would be his successor and announced in May 2025 that he would hand over the CEO job at the end of the year. Abel took over on January 1, 2026; Buffett remains chair.

Forbes lists his home as Des Moines, Iowa, where Berkshire Hathaway Energy is based.

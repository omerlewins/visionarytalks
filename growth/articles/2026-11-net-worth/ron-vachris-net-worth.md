## Who is Ron Vachris?

Roland Michael "Ron" Vachris joined Price Club in 1982 as a part-time forklift driver while taking business classes at Glendale Community College in Arizona. He did not finish a degree. Price Club merged with Costco in 1993, and he stayed through the merger, moving from warehouse manager to regional roles and then to Costco's head office in Issaquah, Washington.

Before becoming CEO he led operations, merchandising and real estate, the three jobs that decide where Costco builds and what it sells. He became president and COO in February 2022 and succeeded Craig Jelinek as CEO on January 1, 2024. He is only the third CEO in the company's history.

As CEO he has kept Costco's formula of low markups and membership fees while adding technology: buy now, pay later online, phone-based scan-and-pay checkout tests, and more digital sales. "Digital and technology are important parts of our future growth," he told Fortune in 2025. He has also said his father's advice shaped his career: don't chase titles or job-hop, stay focused, according to Fortune.

## Who is Phebe Novakovic?

Novakovic is the daughter of an Air Force officer. She graduated from Smith College in 1979 and earned an MBA at Wharton in 1988. From 1997 to 2001 she worked at the CIA and the Defense Department, according to Wikipedia; Forbes describes her as a former CIA operative. She joined General Dynamics in 2001 and ran several of its businesses before becoming president and chief operating officer in 2012.

She became CEO on January 1, 2013, one of the few women to lead a major US defense contractor. Under her, General Dynamics bought CSRA in 2018 for about $9.8 billion. She sits on the boards of JPMorgan Chase and Abbott Laboratories, which is why Forbes lists an Abbott stake alongside her General Dynamics shares. She is married to David Morrison and has three daughters, according to Wikipedia.

### The 10-year award

The October 15, 2026 award is the most important new fact. Nu gave Vélez 13,577,733 restricted Class A shares, registered in his name on the grant date, that vest over 10 years with the last tranche on January 1, 2036. The filing says it replaces his regular annual equity awards for 2027 through 2031. At the $13.29 price on his October 23 filing, the award was worth about $180 million.

The only condition in the filing is that he keeps working at Nu. It sets no stock price or profit targets, unlike the performance-based awards many US founders receive.

## Who is David Vélez?

Vélez was born in Medellín about 45 years ago. When he was nine, his family moved to Costa Rica to get away from the violence of Colombia's drug wars, according to Forbes. He earned a bachelor's degree and an MBA at Stanford, then worked in investment banking at Morgan Stanley, in private equity at General Atlantic and in venture capital at Sequoia Capital.

He started Nubank in 2013 after finding that five big banks controlled about 80% of Brazil's market and charged high fees for basic services, Forbes says. Its no-fee credit card took off, and Nubank now has more than 100 million customers in Brazil, Mexico and Colombia. He lives in São Paulo and is married to Mariel Reyes Milk.

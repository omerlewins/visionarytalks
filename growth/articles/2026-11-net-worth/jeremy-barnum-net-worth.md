## Who is Jeremy Barnum?

Barnum studied chemistry at Harvard College and joined JPMorgan in 1994. He spent most of his career in the corporate and investment bank, where he ran global research and then served as the division's CFO from 2013 to 2021. In May 2021 he replaced Jennifer Piepszak as the firm's chief financial officer.

As CFO he oversees finance, treasury, investor relations and business management, and he is the bank's main voice on its quarterly earnings calls. In January 2026 he defended JPMorgan's plan to spend about $105 billion in 2026 as the cost of staying ahead, and warned that a proposed 10% cap on credit card interest rates would shrink access to credit, Fortune reported. Asked about the long-running question of who will succeed Dimon, he has said succession is "an essential strategic priority."

He lives in New York and is married with two daughters, according to JPMorgan.

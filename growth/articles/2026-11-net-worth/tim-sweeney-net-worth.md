# Tim Sweeney Net Worth 2026: Epic Games' Founder, a Shrinking Valuation and 50,000 Acres of Forest

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked November 24, 2026**

Tim Sweeney's wealth is almost all his stake in Epic Games, which is private and has never disclosed how much of it he owns. Forbes put his net worth at $7.6 billion in May 2022, when Epic was valued at about $32 billion, according to Wikipedia. Disney's $1.5 billion investment in February 2024 valued Epic at $22.5 billion, The Information reported, and Epic laid off more than 1,000 people in March 2026 as Fortnite engagement fell.

Scaling Forbes' 2022 figure to the lower valuation gives about $5.4 billion, so a fair range is $4 billion to $6 billion. He has also bought about 50,000 acres of North Carolina forest, much of it to protect, and given thousands of acres away.

Tencent's 2012 deal is the one that matters most for Sweeney: it bought 48.4% of the shares then issued, equal to 40% of Epic including employee options, for $330 million. Everything after diluted both Tencent and Sweeney. The Disney price was roughly 29% below the 2022 valuation, The Information reported.

## What has happened to Epic since

Fortnite made Epic one of the most valuable private companies in games after 2017. The years since have been harder:

- **September 2023:** Epic cut 870 jobs, sold Bandcamp and spun off SuperAwesome. Sweeney said it was spending more than it earned.
- **March 2026:** Epic announced more than 1,000 more layoffs after Fortnite engagement declined from 2025, aiming for over $500 million of savings, according to Wikipedia.
- **March 2026:** Epic settled with Google, which agreed to cut Play Store fees and allow more alternative billing and app stores, after Epic won its antitrust case at trial in December 2023.
- **October 2026:** The game Epic is building with Disney was delayed past its planned March 2027 release, Bloomberg reported, according to Wikipedia.

No new valuation has been reported since Disney's investment. If one were set now, the layoffs and Fortnite's slowdown suggest it would not be higher than $22.5 billion.

## The land

Since the 2008 property crash, Sweeney has bought large tracts of North Carolina land and become one of the state's largest private landowners, with about 50,000 acres of forest as of November 2025, according to Wikipedia. He paid $15 million for the 7,000-acre Box Creek Wilderness and donated a conservation easement on it to the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service in 2016. In 2021 he announced a gift of 7,500 acres in the Roan Highlands, described as the largest private conservation land donation in the state's history.

Land under conservation easements is worth less on paper, and he has given much of it away, so we do not count it at market value.

## Who is Tim Sweeney?

Timothy Dean Sweeney was born in 1970 and grew up in Potomac, Maryland, the youngest of three brothers, according to Wikipedia. He taught himself to program as a teenager and studied mechanical engineering at the University of Maryland, finishing one credit short of a degree because Epic took over his time.

He wrote the Unreal Engine, which became one of the most widely used game engines, and built Epic into the company behind Fortnite and the Epic Games Store. He led Epic's antitrust fights with Apple and Google over app store fees, and lives in Cary, North Carolina. He was inducted into the Academy of Interactive Arts & Sciences Hall of Fame in 2012.

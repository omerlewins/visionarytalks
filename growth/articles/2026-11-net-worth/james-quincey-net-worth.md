## Who is James Quincey?

Quincey was born in London and spent a few early years in Hanover, New Hampshire, while his father lectured at Dartmouth, before the family settled in Birmingham, England. He studied electronic engineering at the University of Liverpool and started out as a strategy consultant at Bain & Company.

He joined Coca-Cola in 1996 and spent much of the next two decades in Latin America and Europe, including leading the purchase of Mexican juice maker Jugos del Valle. He became president in 2015, CEO in May 2017 and chairman in April 2019. As CEO he cut back the company's long list of weaker brands and pushed into coffee by buying Costa for $5.1 billion in 2019, a chain Coca-Cola has since considered selling, according to BevNET.

In December 2025 Coca-Cola named chief operating officer Henrique Braun, who joined the company the same year Quincey did, as his successor. Quincey became executive chairman on March 31, 2026. He is married to Jacqui Quincey, has two children and lives in London.

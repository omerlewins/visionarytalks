## How much of Reddit does Steve Huffman own?

Reddit has two share classes in use. Class A has one vote and Class B has ten. On March 31, 2026 there were 141,867,916 Class A shares and 50,543,398 Class B shares outstanding.

That last line is why Reddit is legally a "controlled company." Advance, the Newhouse family's company that has owned a big piece of Reddit since 2006, holds 42,191,092 Class B shares and gave Huffman an irrevocable proxy to vote them under a March 19, 2024 agreement. Tencent signed a similar agreement. So Huffman owns under 2% of Reddit's shares but casts three of every four votes.

## Who is Steve Huffman?

Huffman grew up in Virginia and studied computer science at the University of Virginia, where he met Alexis Ohanian. In 2005, as they were finishing college, they pitched Y Combinator a food-ordering app. Paul Graham turned it down but encouraged them to build something else, and they came back with Reddit, which they called "the front page of the internet." Huffman, then 21, wrote the first version himself.

They sold Reddit to Condé Nast in 2006, a year after starting it. Huffman left in 2009 and cofounded the travel search site Hipmunk. He returned as CEO in July 2015, after a period of upheaval at Reddit, and has run it ever since.

As CEO he took Reddit public, signed data licensing deals with Google and OpenAI for AI training, and fought a bruising 2023 battle with moderators over charges for API access. His wealth is now tied to a question bigger than Reddit: whether Google keeps sending searchers to Reddit threads in an age of AI answers.

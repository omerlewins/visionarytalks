# RJ Scaringe Net Worth 2026: Rivian's Founder, a $402 Million Paper Payday and the End of His Supervoting Shares

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked November 24, 2026**

RJ Scaringe's visible wealth is about $89 million: 4.75 million Rivian shares worth about $53 million at the $11.22 close on November 23, 2026, plus about $35 million of in-the-money value in older options. Rivian reported his 2025 pay as $402.6 million, but almost all of it is a 36.5 million-share option that is worth nothing until Rivian trades above $15.22. On November 6, 2025, Rivian cancelled the performance part of his 2021 award, 20,355,946 options at $21.72 that needed the stock to reach far higher prices, and granted the "2025 CEO Award": 36.5 million options at $15.22, the closing price that day. Of those, 22 million vest only if Rivian's stock clears price hurdles, and 14.5 million vest on operating income and cash flow targets, according to the proxy. InsideEVs reported the top price hurdle is $140, which would make the award worth about $4.6 billion to $5 billion.

At $11.22, every one of those options is under water.

## The divorce and the spinoffs

In July 2025, Scaringe transferred Rivian shares and options to his former spouse under a divorce settlement, according to a Form 4 filed July 11, 2025. The Autopian, citing Bloomberg, put the transferred stock and options at about $125 million and said Bloomberg valued his remaining stake at less than $200 million in May 2026, when the stock was near $15.50.

Two Rivian offshoots add to his wealth outside Rivian's share price:

- **Also**, the micromobility company Rivian spun out in 2025, which he chairs. In December 2025, Also bought back 1,352,726 of his Also options at its Series C price to make room for a new investor, and he received about $23.5 million in cash, net, the Rivian proxy says.
- **Mind Robotics**, an industrial AI company he chairs. On November 6, 2025 it gave him 1,000,000 profits-interest units, worth up to a 10% economic interest once its value grows past a threshold. Rivian owned 37.6% of Mind Robotics as of March 31, 2026.

## Who is RJ Scaringe?

Robert Joseph Scaringe was born on January 19, 1983, in Rockledge, Florida, according to Wikipedia. He earned a bachelor's degree from Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute and a master's and PhD in mechanical engineering from MIT's Sloan Automotive Laboratory.

He founded Rivian in 2009, straight out of his doctorate, and spent a decade building it quietly before Amazon and Ford invested. Rivian now sells the R1T pickup, R1S SUV and commercial vans, and is ramping up the cheaper R2. Scaringe has three children from his marriage to Meagan McGone, which ended in 2025.

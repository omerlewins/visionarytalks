## Who is Jay Chaudhry?

Jagtar Singh "Jay" Chaudhry grew up in Panoh, a village in the Himalayan foothills that had no electricity or running water until his school years, according to Forbes. He studied electronics engineering at IIT (BHU) in Varanasi and came to the US in 1980 for graduate school at the University of Cincinnati, where he earned master's degrees in industrial engineering, computer engineering and marketing.

After corporate jobs at IBM, Unisys and NCR, he and his wife Jyoti started SecureIT in 1996 and sold it to VeriSign two years later. He then started CipherTrust, CoreHarbor and AirDefense, all of which were bought, CipherTrust for $274 million. Forbes rates him a self-made billionaire, and his family still owns about a third of Zscaler nearly two decades after he started it.

Zscaler sells "zero trust" security that runs in the cloud instead of in company firewalls. Chaudhry moved from the Bay Area to Reno, Nevada. He has given to IIT (BHU), the American India Foundation and the Sankara Eye Foundation, and paid for a mobile medical lab in his home village, according to Wikipedia.

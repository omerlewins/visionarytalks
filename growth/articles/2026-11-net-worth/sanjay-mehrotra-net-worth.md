## Who is Sanjay Mehrotra?

Mehrotra has spent about 45 years in memory chips and holds more than 70 patents, several of them foundational to high-capacity flash memory, according to Micron's proxy. He co-founded SanDisk in 1988, ran it as CEO from 2011 and sold it to Western Digital in 2016.

Micron hired him as CEO in May 2017 and made him chairman in January 2025. He received the Semiconductor Industry Association's Robert N. Noyce Award in 2023 and sits on the board of CDW. Two of the family trusts listed in Micron's 2025 proxy carry the name of his wife, Sangeeta Mehrotra.

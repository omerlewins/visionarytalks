# Darren Woods Net Worth 2026: ExxonMobil's CEO Holds $311 Million of Stock He Mostly Cannot Sell

**By Daniel Mercer | Checked November 24, 2026**

Darren Woods's visible wealth is about $360 million. His latest Form 4 lists 1,979,489 ExxonMobil shares and units, worth about $311 million at the $156.90 close on November 23, 2026. But about 1.62 million of them are restricted performance shares, and 961,050 cannot be sold until 2031 or after he retires. He also has a pension worth $50.9 million, and Exxon paid him $33.0 million for 2025.

That lock-up is Exxon's design. It pays its CEO mostly in restricted performance shares that unlock over many years, and some stay restricted until he retires. Woods has not sold a single Exxon share on the open market in any Form 4 since 2019; every reduction was shares withheld for tax or small gifts to family trusts.

## Who is Darren Woods?

Darren Wayne Woods was born on December 16, 1965, in Wichita, Kansas, and grew up near U.S. military bases around the world because of his father's work as a military supplier, according to Wikipedia. He earned an electrical engineering degree from Texas A&M and an MBA from Northwestern's Kellogg School.

He joined Exxon in 1992 and spent most of his career in refining and chemicals before succeeding Rex Tillerson as CEO in January 2017, when Tillerson became Secretary of State. He has led Exxon through its push into the Permian Basin and its bet on carbon capture, and through repeated clashes with climate activists and Congress.

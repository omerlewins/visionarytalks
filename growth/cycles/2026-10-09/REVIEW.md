## C1-03 — Costco: sourced short first draft

Proposed path requires full inventory and live-site duplicate checks before reservation. Historical lead: 14,800 US monthly searches / KD 12 from the September briefing, unverified today.

### Who owns Costco?

Costco Wholesale Corporation is a public company owned by shareholders. Its shares trade under the ticker COST. Buying membership gives a customer access to Costco's stores and services; buying common stock makes an investor a shareholder. Those are separate relationships.

Costco's investor site provides information about its common stock and shareholder services. Its fiscal 2026 annual filing directs readers to proxy disclosures for detailed beneficial ownership. A precise “largest owners” table needs its own dated filings; the company's current stock-information page does not provide one.

Sources: [Costco stock information](https://investor.costco.com/stock-info/default.aspx) and [FY2026 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983226000093/cost-20260830.htm).

Complete the article with: dated beneficial-owner table; share versus voting control explanation; sourced founder/history timeline; CEO versus owner distinction; membership versus shareholder FAQ; sources and substantive-update log. Check the latest available proxy and relevant subsequent filings. Do not assume a future proxy has already been filed or claim a fund manager personally owns the shares.

Proposed links: Ownership hub when live; a genuinely relevant existing retailer-ownership article; a future Costco business-model story only after it exists. No numeric stakes, market capitalization or revenue have been invented to fill the draft.

# Leaders editorial standard

The [Leaders Content Playbook](PLAYBOOK.md) supersedes earlier template guidance. See [implementation and remaining research gates](PLAYBOOK_AUDIT.md).

Use dated HTML tables for every supported personal estimate. Show a chart only with at least three dated observations. Separate estimators, and preserve missing values as null. Company valuations, funding, stake valuations and transaction consideration are separate measures. Keep source access dates and actual valuation/publication/observation dates explicit.

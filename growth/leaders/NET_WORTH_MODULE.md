# Concise leader net-worth module

Applies to every leader profile. Heading: “[Name] net worth”.

- One paragraph, typically 60–90 words: amount or supported range, observation date, wealth source, financial turning point and the most consequential unknown. Source and company links flow inside the sentences.
- A compact graphic and 2–4 dated timeline entries. Label each event's measure. A company financing or transaction event does not become a personal net-worth observation.
- A visible methodology note of roughly 60–90 words: explain our independent public-record research, sources checked, calculation or estimate basis, observation date and material limitations. Full inputs and claim mapping stay in the evidence ledger.
- Graph choice follows evidence: two or more comparable personal-wealth observations permit dated points with gaps; one permits a snapshot; a supported modeled range permits a range graphic with assumptions. No fabricated historical points, interpolated values or company valuations on a personal-wealth axis.
- If wealth cannot be supported, paragraph says “not publicly established” and explains the missing stake or asset evidence. Use a sourced event timeline; omit a numeric graph rather than plot zero.
- Distinguish source-checked reported facts, third-party estimates and our calculated equity scenarios. Valuation × verified stake is a gross equity scenario, not a complete net-worth estimate.
- Each value stores measure, currency, amount/range, effective date, basis, source URL and source-check date. A reader-facing “verified” badge requires a reviewer record; checking a source does not audit private finances.

Pilot: michael-truell-net-worth.json supplies the paragraph, one estimate, two event entries and methodology. Preview renders a zero-based snapshot on a $0–$3bn axis, explicitly labeled as an estimate. The August event carries no plotted wealth amount. No historical wealth change is claimed.

Apply this requirement to Michael Truell, Anton Osika, Dario Amodei, Aravind Srinivas, Maor Shlomo and Sam Altman. Current figures for the five later candidates must be researched before drafting their modules.



## Chart consistency and independent research

Every leader graphic uses the homepage EditorialChart design: shared ink/wash/rule and font tokens, vertical columns, light horizontal gridlines, zero baseline, date labels, source caption and accessible data table. The prototype generates homepage-chart.css from the actual app chart styles on each build. Production implementation must reuse EditorialChart rather than introduce a separate wealth-chart theme.

Methodology describes the independent research actually conducted: reviewing public documents, comparing dates and definitions, tracing transaction status and reproducing calculations. Say that we conduct independent research into public records. Identify third-party estimates separately; never imply an independent audit, private balance-sheet access, new interview or an original valuation that was not performed.

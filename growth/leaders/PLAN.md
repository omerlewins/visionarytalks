## Content architecture

For each AI company, build three connected answers: **ownership → leader → money**. Add salary coverage where our wage dataset supports it. Company pages explain legal structure, control and investors. Leader pages explain the person, career, role and dated wealth evidence. Money pages explain comparable company metrics. These are distinct reader jobs, not three rewrites of the same story.

Start with one page per important person covering both “who is” and “net worth.” Pilot title: **Who Is Michael Truell? Cursor's Co-Founder and His Net Worth**. Proposed canonical: `/michael-truell-net-worth/`. Check the full CMS/live inventory before reservation. Do not create a second thin `/who-is-michael-truell/` article. A URL may be stable while the title and dated estimate change.

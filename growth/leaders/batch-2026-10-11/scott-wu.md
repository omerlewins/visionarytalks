## The official score behind the coding story

The [official 2014 IOI results](https://stats.ioinformatics.org/results/2014) list Scott Wu with a gold medal and 600 points out of 600. He tied for first with Ishraq Huda and Yinzhan Xu. Steven Hao, another Cognition founder, appears sixth with 525 points. This is a dated competition record, not a popularity-based reputation claim.

The financially relevant question is how a technical network becomes a founding team. People who have worked or competed together may already understand one another’s capabilities. That can reduce uncertainty during formation, but it does not determine how equity is allocated. A cap table can reflect capital, timing, vesting or negotiated contributions.

The brief contains conflicting birth years. We omit an exact age rather than selecting the more common claim. Age does not explain the current business model or ownership. This profile concentrates on the company’s dated announcements and the limits of its publicly described founder economics.

## How Cognition describes the software-engineering bet

[Cognition’s Series E announcement](https://cognition.com/blog/series-e) dates the company’s start to 2024. It describes a strategy in which engineers direct agents and delegate execution. That is the company’s account of its purpose. It does not independently establish that an agent can complete every engineering task reliably.

Our analysis is that the relevant unit is completed useful work. A coding demonstration measures a narrower result than maintaining a production system. Customers must consider review effort, incident risk and how the tool operates within existing processes. The business case depends on the value of completed work after those costs.

This also changes what a financial profile should investigate. A fast-growing usage metric may show activity, but monetization and retention remain separate questions. A revenue run-rate can indicate momentum without supplying recognized annual revenue, gross margin or cash generation. We preserve the company’s metric labels.

## The capital history is unusually clear

[Wu’s September 8, 2025 funding post](https://cognition.com/blog/funding-growth-and-the-next-frontier-of-ai-coding-agents) says Cognition raised over $400 million at $10.2 billion post-money. The [September 2026 announcement](https://cognition.com/blog/series-e) says it raised over $2 billion at $48 billion. These are corporate financing observations from interested company sources.

Our calculation: the two disclosed rounds total more than $2.4 billion. This is a lower bound for those observations, not lifetime capital or cash proceeds to Wu. The word “over” matters. Converting it into an exact $2.4 billion would make the ledger appear more precise than its sources.

The later announcement says run-rate revenue rose from $492 million in May to almost $900 million. We keep “run-rate” and “almost” in the table. We do not turn the observation into audited annual revenue. The financing valuation divided by a run-rate would also be a valuation multiple, not a profit multiple.

## Unequal founder economics remain unexplained

The [Forbes Türkiye report](https://www.forbes.com.tr/milyarderler/cognition-yeni-yapay-zeka-milyarderini-yaratti) says Steven Hao and Walden Yan had larger stakes than Wu at the period it examined. It also says the reason was unknown and the company declined to comment on wealth. That limitation is part of the finding.

The CEO title does not imply the largest shareholding. Leadership responsibility and negotiated ownership are different variables. We do not explain the difference with speculation about who wrote more code, who invested first or who joined earliest. The public source does not establish those causes.

We also do not multiply Wu’s historical estimate by the rise in company valuation. That would assume his percentage, security terms, other assets and the provider’s methodology remained unchanged. The financing record supports a company timeline. It does not support a new personal wealth series.

## From a solved problem to an engineering business

A competition gives a precise score because the task and scoring rules are defined. A commercial software project usually has less tidy boundaries. The code can compile while failing a customer requirement. Tests can pass while an integration remains fragile. The competition record establishes Wu’s performance under its own rules; it cannot establish the economics of Devin.

Our analysis is that the founding team’s technical familiarity is useful when paired with a repeatable delivery process. The relevant questions are who specifies the task, who checks the result, and who remains responsible when an agent makes a mistake. Those responsibilities can determine whether automation saves money after supervision. They also explain why a company needs more than a successful demonstration to build recurring customer demand.

The [2025 financing announcement](https://cognition.com/blog/funding-growth-and-the-next-frontier-of-ai-coding-agents) describes Devin ARR rising from $1 million in September 2024 to $73 million in June 2025. Cognition says this was before acquiring Windsurf. This distinction prevents attributing the earlier increase to an acquired business. ARR remains a company-reported rate, rather than a statement of profits or Wu’s personal income.

## What the financial record reveals

Cognition provides a useful example of why founder wealth should be investigated separately from executive rank. The historical reporting describes unequal stakes, while the newer announcement establishes a larger company financing value. Neither explains the allocation mechanism.

The next decisive evidence would be a dated ownership schedule or a new personal estimate with a transparent methodology. Until then, the article retains the historical $600 million observation and visibly labels it historical. The absence of a current figure should not be hidden in a chart caption.

## Independent research and methodology

We preserve lower-bound financing language and revenue run-rate wording. We do not assign a cause to unequal founder stakes or extrapolate a current estimate.

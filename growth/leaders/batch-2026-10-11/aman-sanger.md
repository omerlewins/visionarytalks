## The product bet was control of the workflow

The team’s interview describes Cursor as a code editor rather than merely an extension. The relevant claim is that controlling the editing environment allowed more changes to the workflow. We attribute that account to the founders. It does not prove the product’s superiority across all use cases.

Our analysis is that an editor creates repeated opportunities to help at the moment a developer works. A suggestion can be accepted, rejected or revised within an existing task. The economic value depends on useful assistance after review, not just the number of generated lines. The interface is part of the product’s business logic.

There is also a tradeoff. Controlling more of the workflow can create differentiation, but increases responsibility for reliability and user experience. A tool that disrupts a trusted editing routine can impose switching costs on customers. Its success requires those costs to be outweighed by practical benefits.

## Read the closing filing in separate buckets

The [SpaceX 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026056945/spcx-20260814.htm) says Anysphere became a wholly owned subsidiary on August 14, 2026. It identifies 389,289,254 Class A shares for outstanding Cursor common and preferred shares, based on $60 billion implied equity value. That is an aggregate corporate transaction input.

The filing separately identifies 1,752,426 shares relating to vested restricted-stock units. It also describes assumed unvested awards and options. These are separate categories, not a single pool that can automatically be divided among four founders. The employee-award categories matter when reading the total consideration.

Our calculation: the two disclosed categories of issued shares sum to 391,041,680. This is the sum of shareholder consideration and vested-RSU consideration in the filing. It excludes the separately assumed unvested awards and options. It is not Sanger’s share count or a measure of his personal net worth.

## The asset changes even when the founder story continues

An all-stock transaction changes what a former shareholder owns. The founder’s economic exposure can move from a private software company to securities in the acquiring company. That makes an old percentage of Cursor an incomplete description of the current asset. The filing does not establish every founder’s personal allocation.

Forbes supplies a closing-value estimate for each cofounder, but we keep that estimate separate from the current total. A closing value is dated to a transaction. A current personal estimate can reflect later market movements, other assets and the provider’s assumptions. We do not silently relabel the closing figure as cash proceeds.

The proposed Bloomberg comparison requires its original dated methodology. Applying two unverified Cursor percentages to the aggregate share pool would appear rigorous while ignoring security classes, award categories and allocation terms. We exclude that model. The filing is more valuable when its limits remain visible.

## What the consideration categories prevent us from assuming

The closing filing is particularly valuable because its categories prevent a common shortcut. Shareholders, vested award holders and unvested award holders have different positions. An aggregate share pool does not become a founder allocation simply because four names are associated with the company. A proper ledger follows each security into the merger.

Our analysis is that a completed acquisition also changes the question a founder profile should ask. The old company value can explain a transaction. The asset retained afterward determines subsequent exposure. A historical Cursor percentage cannot be rolled forward without the exchange terms and the actual securities delivered to that holder.

Sanger’s dated personal estimate should therefore be read beside the corporate filing, rather than replaced by arithmetic from it. One gives a provider’s modeled total. The other documents aggregate legal consideration. Agreement between two headline values would not establish the provider’s exact method. Disagreement would not identify an error automatically.
